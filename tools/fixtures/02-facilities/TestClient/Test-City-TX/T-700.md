# T-700 (fixture) — VERIFIED-FORMAT

`verified` holds a prose sentence rather than a date. Lint must flag VERIFIED-FORMAT on
this file.

The field answers exactly one question — when was this card last checked against a primary
source — so it takes a date or the literal `never`, nothing else. This fixture's value is
modelled on a real one: before the 2026-08-21 migration, ten live cards carried prose here,
one of them forty words about a 1976 BOM cross-check, and two carried two dates apiece.

The root cause is worth keeping in view, because it is why the rule exists rather than a
convention: `verified` was **absent from the exemplar's frontmatter block entirely** while
OP-FRONTMATTER already lint-checked its *presence*. A required field that nothing specified
is one people will fill in reasonably and differently. Presence checks do not imply format.

Correct handling: `verified: 2026-07-23` here, and the provenance detail — including what
was pointedly *not* verified — as a `**Verification:**` paragraph under `## Notes`.
