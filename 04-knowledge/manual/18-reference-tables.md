## 18.6 Launcher and receiver dimensions

Dimensions in inches, RF flanges.

| Size | Valve handle swing radius | Lid swing radius | C | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3" | 24" | 12" | 16" | 42" ± |
| 4" | 24" | 12" | 16" | 46" ± |
| 6" | 36" | 12" | 20" | 63" ± |
| 8" | 36" | 24" | 36" | 82" ± |
| 10" | 42" | 24" | 40" | 120" ± |
| 12" | 42" | 24" | 40" | 120" ± |

Access requirements: more than 36" working clearance, and the working position within approximately 48" of grade or deck.

**Connection ratings.** Sizes 3", 4", 6", and 8" carry 300# connections on barrel and valve. Sizes 10" and 12" carry 150#. This convention is consistent across the industry. Where the heater flange differs in size or rating, the customer fabricates the adapter.
