# 16. Documentation and Deliverables

**Layer:** 04-knowledge/manual

## 16.1 Before mobilization

**Heater-specific execution SOP.** Numbered `SOP-DCK-[HeaterTag]-[NNN]`, prepared from confirmed heater data and issued to the customer before mobilization. It states the actual circuit configuration, tube dimensions and metallurgy per section, connection details, operating parameters, pig progression sequence, phase-by-phase procedure, and any facility-specific requirement. The SOP governs execution; this manual describes the method it applies.

**Process flow diagram.** Numbered `PFD-DCK-[job]-REV[N]`. Shows the pigging circuit from pumper through launcher, coil, receiver, and diverter to the tanks, with equipment blocks and every connection annotated by size and type. Where filtration is elected, the filtration loop appears as a second process on the same diagram; where it is not, the diagram shows the actual effluent route instead. The diagram reflects the elected configuration rather than a generic one.

**Data confirmation table.** Where heater data was extracted from drawings or data sheets, the extracted values are presented back to the customer for confirmation before the SOP is drafted. Pig sizing derives from these figures.

**Pre-execution technical package.** Where the customer's engineering review requires it, the SOP and PFD are issued as part of a fuller package comprising equipment data sheets for the pumping unit, filter press, and launchers and receivers; chemical data for any additive or solution in use; the risk assessment and job safety analysis; and applicable certifications.

## 16.2 During execution

Each circuit carries a running field record through the work, capturing pigs run by size and type, direction of travel, operating pressure and flow, effluent clarity and discharge duration, pig condition on recovery, and any anomaly together with what was done about it. The record continues unbroken across shift handovers.

Flow test data is recorded at the two defined points: the baseline before the first launch, and the post-cleaning test after the final pass.

Where a plug header hang-up occurs, the event is documented with pig size, direction of travel, pressure and flow response, and what was required to recover the pig.

## 16.3 At completion

**Flow test record.** Before and after figures per circuit, at matched flow rate, with the pressure differential that demonstrates the improvement. This is the primary quantitative deliverable.

**Completion record.** Per circuit, confirming the three completion criteria were met, together with the pig progression actually run and the final pig size achieved.

**Field records.** The running record described above, covering the work performed on each circuit.

**Waste disposition.** Confirmation of the streams generated and the designated routes to which they were sent.

Where the scope included an inspection run, the inspection data and its interpretation are the vendor's deliverable, not USADebusk's. USADebusk's record covers the propulsion parameters held during the run.

## 16.4 Document control

Documents are revision-controlled. Each carries its document number, revision, preparation date, and the identity of the preparer, and each revision supersedes the one before it. Where a document is revised after issue, the revision is reissued rather than amended in place.

Job documents are heater-specific and their requirements apply to the heater named on them. A document prepared for one heater is not applied to another.

## 16.5 Reference documents

This manual, `USAD-MAN-PIG-001`, may be referenced from a job SOP or a proposal as the methodology reference. Where a job SOP and this manual describe the same activity, the job SOP governs.

Additional standalone procedures exist for specific contingencies and are provided on request.
