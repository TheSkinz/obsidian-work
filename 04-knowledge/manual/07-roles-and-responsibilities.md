Three parties are involved in a decoking scope. The boundaries below are the standard division and apply on every job unless the contract states otherwise. They are stated early and explicitly because most scope friction on this work traces to one of these lines being assumed rather than agreed.

## 7.2 USADebusk scope

USADebusk provides the complete pigging system: pumping units, launchers and receivers, hoses and connections, pigs, and the crew to operate them. USADebusk makes and breaks all surface connections between its own equipment and the customer's flanges, operates the circuits, manages the pig progression, monitors and records operating parameters, performs the before and after flow tests, and produces the job SOP, process flow diagram, and completion documentation.

Where filtration is elected, USADebusk provides and operates the filter press and transfer pump.

## 7.3 Customer scope

The customer prepares the coil and the site. That means process isolation and positive blinding, protection or isolation of relief devices, confirmation that the coil is drained, cooled, and free of process fluid, and issue of the permit to work.

The customer supplies water to the USADebusk tanks and designates the effluent disposal route, holding the environmental permitting for it.

The customer fabricates flange adapters wherever the heater connection size or rating differs from the launcher, and fabricates jumper spools where passes are to be looped. Both are identified during engineering so fabrication is complete before mobilization; discovering an adapter requirement at rig-in is the single most common cause of avoidable standing time on this work.

The customer provides scaffolding where launcher or receiver positions are elevated and lack a sound working deck with room to change pigs. Elevated positions that already have a suitable deck need none.

## 7.4 Lifting contractor scope

All rigging and lifting is performed by the customer's lifting contractor. USADebusk does not perform lifts under any circumstances. Where equipment placement, launcher installation, or spool handling requires a lift, it is scheduled with the lifting contractor as part of the rig-in plan.

## 7.5 Other scopes in the same unit

A decoking scope frequently runs alongside other work in the same heater or unit, sometimes performed by other divisions of the same contractor and sometimes placed under a single purchase order for the customer's convenience. Those are separate scopes with their own procedures and their own supervision. This manual and the job SOP cover the mechanical decoking scope only.

## 7.6 Approval gates

Two points in the job require the customer's positive confirmation before work proceeds past them.

The first is release of the coil under permit, which precedes any connection. The second applies on jobs with an inspection component: where a smart pig or in-line inspection run follows mechanical cleaning, the customer's written acceptance of the inspection data is required before dewatering, hose disconnection, or any rig-out activity begins. Rig-out ahead of that acceptance forecloses a re-run.
