# Service: Plenum (USA Debusk)

USA Debusk learning portal on Plenum. Credentials through Secure Forms only — never put passwords in chat or files.

## Sign-in

- Entry URL: `https://usadebusk.plenumapps.com/start` (signed-out redirects to `/?msg=not-logged-in` with login modal already open).
- Modal heading: “Login or sign up to start learning.”
- Fields visible together: **What's your e-mail?** (placeholder E-mail) and **Your password?** (placeholder Password). Submit: **Login**.
- Also on the modal: Create Account, Forgot your password?, and SSO options (Google, Facebook, LinkedIn, Apple, Sign in with SSO USAD OAuth). Prefer email/password unless Jesse asks for SSO.
- One Secure Form with email + password (both visible). After fill, click **Login** yourself.
- After success: `https://usadebusk.plenumapps.com/start` (title After Login) — enroll/course list and stats.

## After login

Stop on `/start` unless Jesse directs otherwise. A NPS-style survey modal (“How likely are you to recommend this course to your co-worker?”) may appear — leave it unless he says to dismiss or answer it.

## Completing a course (when Jesse asks)

- Scrubbing/seeking a Wistia video to the end often does **not** unlock the unit. Use real playback. Playback-rate menu may offer up to **2x** — use the highest rate Jesse allows.
- Captions/transcript may exist in Video settings but often have no track (Subtitles = None only).
- Before quizzes: look for downloadable course resources (PDFs/handouts) — they can contain answers.
- Facility-specific rules can differ from generic OSHA; prefer on-page and downloaded course materials over generic OSHA knowledge.
- Acknowledgments: when he asked to finish, check boxes and continue.

## Not this service

This is not ISNetworld, not EHS Insight (`debusksg.ehsinsight.com`), and not the Empower phone app.
