# Service: EHS Insight (Debusk SG)

USADebusk Safety Gateway / EHS Insight hiring-client portal. Same lane as ISNetworld-style LMS work. Credentials through Secure Forms only — never put passwords or codes in chat or files.

## Sign-in

- Entry URL: `https://debusksg.ehsinsight.com/site#/home` (redirects to `/login?returnurl=/site#/home` when signed out).
- Brand on login: USADebusk. Page title: EHS Insight. Heading: Sign In.
- **Step 1:** field **Username**, button **Continue**. No password field until Continue.
- **Step 2:** Username becomes disabled (edit pencil to change it). Field **Password**, button **Continue**.
- **Step 3 (2FA):** message "We sent a Two-Factor Code to your registered phone number." Field **Two-Factor Code**, button **Continue**. Also shows **Disable Two-Factor Authentication** — do not click unless Jesse asks.
- One Secure Form per step (only fields visible now). After each fill, click **Continue** yourself; do not assume Enter submitted. On the OTP step, Enter alone can leave "Two-Factor Code is required" even when the box looks filled — click **Continue**.
- No SSO buttons seen. ListCredentials first; if no password manager, use Secure Forms.
- After success the app lands briefly on `#/home` then settles on `https://debusksg.ehsinsight.com/site#/dashboard`.

## After login

Stop on My Dashboard unless Jesse directs otherwise. Do not launch courses, acknowledgments, or settings until he says so.

Signed-in header shows company USADebusk and user name (e.g. Utsey, Jesse Wayne). Left nav includes Dashboard, My Tasks, My Acknowledgments, My Career, Safety Data Sheets, Forms, Documents, Reports, Help.

## Not this service

This is not ISNetworld (`isnetworld.com`) and not the Empower phone app.
