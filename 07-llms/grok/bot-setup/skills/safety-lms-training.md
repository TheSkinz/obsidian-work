# Safety LMS online training (browser)

Use when completing assigned safety / hiring-client LMS courses in the box browser — ISNetworld Complete Online Training and similar sites Jesse names later (other contractor safety portals with video modules, quizzes, and acknowledgments).

## Scope

- Empower (joinempower.com) is phone-only for workers; do not expect a usable worker dashboard at `app.joinempower.com` (often a Firebase Invalid Dynamic Link).
- ISNetworld path: sign in at `https://www.isnetworld.com/Login`, then **Complete Online Training** (often `https://www.isnetworld.com/contractortraining/workers`). Filter by hiring client and site/requirement. Prefer assigned Launch/resume over empty general library searches.
- For a new site: open the URL Jesse gives, find assigned/available courses, and use the speed-through playbook below unless the page blocks it.

## Sign-in

- Prefer 1Password Shared with Grok Bot when connected; otherwise in-chat forms one step at a time. Never ask for passwords in chat text.
- Username-first then password is common on ISNetworld; follow whatever the live page shows.
- Sessions can drop after finishing a course. Expect to re-auth mid-batch when completing multiple courses.
- Keep one browser tab when possible; wait through long post-login spinners before assuming failure.

## Speeding through a course (when the user wants it finished)

Only launch/complete a course when the user explicitly asked for that course (or “finish the rest” of a named set).

1. **Videos:** Skip with **Continue** / Next when allowed. Do not wait for playback; you cannot hear audio.
2. **Long pages:** Scroll down to unlock or reveal Continue faster.
3. **Quizzes:** Answer from on-screen course text, not blind guesses.
4. **Responsibilities / acknowledgments:** When the user has said to finish the course (or said they have read them), check the acknowledgment boxes and continue. Stop if the step needs a photo, wet signature, or personal attestation only they can make.
5. Stay on the named course only — do not launch sibling trainings without ask.

## After each course

- Confirm status on the list (Complete / In Progress), any score or pass mark, expiration, and certificate/print if shown.
- Quote site labels; never invent completion, scores, or Employee IDs.
- If logged out, re-auth and continue the remaining list rather than reporting partial failure as final.

## Do not

- Submit training or mark complete without an explicit ask for that action.
- Change profile, link Employee IDs, accept unrelated terms, or send messages unless asked.
- Assume Empower web covers QuickCheck / digital ISN ID — that stays on the phone app unless Jesse provides another path.
