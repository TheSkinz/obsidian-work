## Document numbering

- **DSP#:** Quote number. Assigned at proposal stage. Format: YYNNN (e.g., DSP26001). Proposal workups filed in Jason's SharePoint folder.
- **USA#:** Job number. Assigned upon award. Format: USAYYNNN (e.g., USA26001). All job execution documents filed under this number in Pigging Jobs folder on SharePoint.
- **CND#:** Job number for Canadian work (DeBusk Services Canada). Format: CNDYYNNN (e.g., CND25004, CND26001). Same role as USA# — it is the job-number series, not a different scheme. **`CAD#` is a misspelling, not a second series** (Jesse, 2026-08-21). Expect it in source documents anyway: the CND25004 ticket-breakdown header carried both "CND25002" and "CAD25002" as stale numbers, and this vault itself filed CND26001 as "CAD26001" until 2026-08-21. Read `CAD` as `CND` and correct it at ingest.
