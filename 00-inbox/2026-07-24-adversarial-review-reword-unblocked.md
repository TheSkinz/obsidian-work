# adversarial-review + idea-triage reword — examined, closed with no change

**Disposition: no change made. The premise was wrong.**

The held item claimed both skills carry self-verification scaffolding that Opus 5 makes
redundant — "double-check" instructions causing extra passes rather than catching anything.
Both files were read in full at close-out on 2026-07-24. **Neither contains an instruction of
that shape.** No double-check, no re-verify pass, no re-read-your-work step. The reword had no
subject.

What `adversarial-review` actually carries is a VERIFIED/ASSERTED tagging discipline, and it is
the opposite of redundant — it exists specifically to stop agreement being mistaken for
verification, stating outright that one agent agreeing with another's unverified read is
"agreement, not verification." Deleting it would remove the only thing preventing three
confident agents from laundering a shared misreading into a confirmed finding. `idea-triage`'s
three verification-flavoured lines are the tentative-duplicate rule, the prior-art check before
dismissal (see [[feedback-power-user-prior-art]] reasoning — dismissal without that check has
burned before), and an anti-fabrication guard on the closing tally. Two input checks and an
honesty guard. Nothing to strip.

**The distinction worth keeping** if this is ever reopened: what the model makes redundant is
re-checking *work already done* — it reconciles its own arithmetic unprompted. What it does not
make redundant is verifying a *premise* against its authoritative source. Drop "verify your
output," keep "verify your inputs."

**The part worth remembering.** This item was itself an instance of the failure it described. It
was a summary written *about* the skills rather than derived from them, and it was wrong — the
fifth such case in the Opus 5 battery, alongside the SOP standard, the F3 baseline, the duration
basis, and a piece of wording introduced the same morning. Every one of those was a confident
secondhand description of a system that did not match the system. See
[[2026-07-24-fixtures-work-better-as-rule-audit]].
