# CND26001 — work the duration both ways before the looping decision lands

Offered at close-out 2026-08-21 and not taken up; filed so it does not evaporate. **Time-boxed: mob is 2026-08-25.**

**The opening.** [[7-1-F-1]]'s Pass looping row records that Syncrude **may loop 8 passes down to 4 circuits** on CND26001, undecided as of 2026-08-20. Until the geometry was rebuilt from drawings on 2026-08-20 nobody could price either layout, because the card's footage was wrong by a factor of eight. It is now right — **2,237 ft per pass, 17,893 ft and 376 tubes for the heater** — so both layouts are computable today.

**What to compute.** Unlooped 8-pass against looped 4-circuit at ~4,474 ft per circuit, with 2 Trimax: per-coil hours at the applicable derated rate, pass-set layout by mode, rig-over count (`ceil(passes ÷ mode) − 1`), and the rig-in/rig-out tier each layout implies — looping changes hose count and therefore the tier, which is the driver the DQ-009 correction added. Apply the coil round-up then the allowance, in that order, per the estimating skill.

**Why it is worth doing before the decision rather than after.** Jesse mobs 08-25 as night operator. Having both numbers in hand means the looping election can be argued on duration at the time it is made, on site, rather than reconstructed afterwards. This heater's history is dominated by standby — 36 hrs in 2024, ~192 hrs in 2025, both waiting on the plant — so duration arguments have real leverage here.

**Caveat to carry.** The 100 ft/hr benchmark is a per-pig rate and this is a vacuum-service oil-sands heater; check the rollup's routine band (47–259 ft/hr per pig, mean ≈99) and the per-coilset outlier rule added 2026-08-21 before settling on a rate. CND25004's own sets ran 47 / 64 / 62 ft/hr, and the 48-hr set is the quarantined outlier — estimate off the 35 and 36 hr sets that cluster.
