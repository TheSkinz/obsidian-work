# Quest markets mechanical cleaning, not just inspection

**Found on a first competitor pass, public sources only, 2026-09-07.** Everything below is what
Quest Integrity **says** on its own pages — marketing copy, attributed, never verified capability.

**ADCV is marketed as *"combined mechanical cleaning plus ultrasonic cleanliness verification"***
(ADCV services page, ADCV case-study PDF, refining page). **FTIS** is marketed separately as
furnace-tube ultrasonic smart-pig inspection with fitness-for-service and remaining-life assessment.
The services index and the refining page **place ADCV before in-line inspection** in sequence.

## Why it is worth a note rather than a shrug

The vault already records Quest as *"a competitor with their own decoking division"*
(`7-1-F-1.md:397`) — so this is corroboration, not news. What is new is the **shape of the public
offer**: cleaning and verification sold as one bundle, with cleaning first.

**That is the same bundle USADebusk splits across itself and an inspection vendor** — and on shared
jobs Quest *is* that inspection vendor, watching the cleaning work at close range.

Two further claims worth knowing: HDS materials claim **manifold access for cleaning and FTIS
without header removal**; and Baker Hughes' 2022 acquisition announcement, as carried by
*Hydrocarbon Processing*, **names Invista and FTIS but not ADCV**.

## What this is NOT

**It does not explain [[DSP26058]].** That quote is recorded `lost-reason: competitor` with the
winner unnamed, and nothing here identifies them. Attributing that loss to Quest would be exactly
the inference the standing rule forbids — competitor outcomes are unknowable, and a plausible
connection is not evidence.

## Open

Whether ADCV field crews are Quest employees, subcontractors, or a mix — the public copy does not
settle it, and it bears directly on whether they can actually execute cleaning at scale or resell
it. And whether any 2024–2026 press or job posting on mechanical decoking capacity sits behind a
gate this pass did not cross.
