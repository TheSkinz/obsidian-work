## Field Notes

### USA26005 — January 2026

**Pigs Ran:** Worked up through the sizes over two pigging shifts — 3.8" and 4" pins first,
then 4.05" and 4.125" pins, with 4" and 4.125" HRs and 4" foams. Cleaning was called complete
at the shift change ending the second pigging shift. Final clean ID 4.026".

**Execution:** Both looped paths ran simultaneously — passes 2+4 and 1+3. Fill and flush
preceded first pig. Cleaning ran materially faster than quoted: 29 pigging hours against 24
quoted, but with rig-in coming in at 10.5 against 12 and no separate rig-out, the heater's
productive total was 39.5 hours against a 40-hour estimate.

**Obstacles:** No coil obstructions, stuck pigs, or fouling surprises recorded. Every hour of
delay on this heater was plant-side, and all 40.5 stand-by hours were consumed before the
first pig moved — 9 hrs waiting to bring equipment into the unit and for heater prep, 12 hrs
overnight (4 hrs permit wait, then 8 hrs after the night shift was evacuated for a scaffolding
incident), 12 hrs the following day held in the laydown yard for an FHR safety audit, 4.5 hrs
more waiting for that audit to close, and 3 hrs for the permit to begin pigging.

**Facility Procedures:** See [[_facility]] — badging, contractor safety audit, and permit lead
time all cost real hours here and should be planned for, not treated as exceptions.
