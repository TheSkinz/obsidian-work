## Site Safety and Procedures

FHR runs contractor safety audits during execution and will hold work in the laydown yard
until the audit closes. On USA26005 this consumed 16.5 hours across a day and night shift
before rig-in was permitted.

Site-wide incidents stop contractor work regardless of involvement. On USA26005 a scaffolding
incident elsewhere in the unit evacuated the night shift, which was sent back to the hotel for
8 hours.

Permit issuance ahead of both rig-in and first pig ran slow enough to be a material schedule
factor — expect to build permit wait into the plan rather than treating it as an exception.
