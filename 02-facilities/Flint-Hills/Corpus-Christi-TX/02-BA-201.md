## Field Notes

### USA26005 — January 2026

**Pigs Ran:** Short ladder — 6" foams, 6" pins, then 6.25" pins to finish. Coils A/B and C/D
pigged until clean. Final clean ID 6.065".

**Execution:** Equipment rigged over from 01-BA-105 in 3 hours, then fill and flush the same
evening. Both looped paths ran simultaneously. Pigging took 9 hours overnight, smart pigging
began at 04:00 and ran 3 hours to shift change, then 2 more the following morning while
waiting on Quest's inspection results for the coils. Rig-out and press cleaning took 7 hours.

This heater beat its estimate substantially — 10 pigging hours against 32 quoted. It ran
cleaner than the 6" bore and 4-coil count suggested, and the whole heater closed out in 25
productive hours against 48 quoted.

**Obstacles:** None. No stand-by, no obstructions, no fouling surprises.

**Facility Procedures:** See [[02-facilities/Flint-Hills/Corpus-Christi-TX/_facility]].
