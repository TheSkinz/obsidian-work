## Heaters at This Facility

**Plant 7 runs two Bitumen Column Feed Heaters, side by side: `7-1 F-1` and `7-2 F-1`.** They are the same
design, built together ("one heater shown — two heaters req'd" on the Foster Wheeler GA).

- [[7-1-F-1|7-1 F-1 Column Feed Heater]] — the heater USADebusk has worked (CND24002 April 2024, CND25004 Sept 2025)
- **7-2 F-1** — sister heater, no card yet. Not worked by USADebusk on any recorded job.

### Naming — settled 2026-08-20

The second heater is **`7-2 F-1`**, never "7-1 F-2". Resolved from the Foster Wheeler plan section's own note,
which states it three times: *"1. HEATER ITEM 7-1F-1 AS SHOWN. 2. HEATER ITEM 7-2F-1 IDENTICAL EXCEPT FOR
ORIENTATION OF PLENUMS, FLOOR ACCESS DOORS & STAIRWAYS (SEE KEY PLAN ON DWG #2). 3. RESULTANT MOVEMENTS AT
OUTLET TUBES WILL BE OPP HAND FOR HTR 7-2F-1."*

**Why the form is what it is.** The drawing-number block is `PLANT | TRAIN | SIZE | SUBJECT | SEQUENCE`,
reading PLANT `7`, TRAIN `1` on `7 1 D-F-015`. So `7-1` and `7-2` are **trains**, each carrying one furnace
`F-1`. "7-1 F-2" would mean a second furnace inside train 1, which no drawing describes and which does not
exist. This is the fact that keeps getting lost and re-litigated — it cost this vault a heater-card rename in
July and a "phantom heater" ruling that was wrong.

**The two are mirror images.** Identical coil geometry; plenums, floor access doors and stairways are
oriented opposite, and outlet-tube movements are opposite hand. Anything positional taken off the 7-1 F-1
drawings — launcher access, stair routes, which side the outlets fall on — flips on 7-2 F-1. The process
side does not.

**Expect a third spelling in Syncrude's own systems.** Their CMS/work-order records use an `F-001` form —
the supplied outlet-spool drawing is annotated `7-2F-001`, and the Plant 37-1 work order used `37-1F-001`.
Same heater, different register: `7-2 F-1` on engineering drawings, `7-2F-001` in the maintenance system.
