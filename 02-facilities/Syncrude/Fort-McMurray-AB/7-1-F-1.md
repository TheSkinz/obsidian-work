**Single bore throughout — 6.625" OD × 0.280" wall, 6.065" ID, every tube, both sections.** No telescoping,
no multiple tube sizes. Confirmed on the Quest title block and on every line of the coil revamp BOM.

**Tube geometry notes.**

**Source.** Quest Integrity isometric `7-1F-1.tif` Rev 1 title block (read from a zoomed crop of
`syncrude drawings.pdf` p1, bottom-left quadrant): `Outer Diameter: 6.625 in (168.3mm)` · `Inner Diameter:
6.065 in (154.1mm)` · `Wall Thickness: 0.280 in (7.1mm)` · `Material: 5Cr-½Mo, Incoloy 825` · `Service:
Bitumen Column Feed Heater` · `Tube Count: Conv. 16, Rad. 31` · `Tube Lengths: Conv. 65.0 ft (19.8m), Rad.
38.6 ft (11.8m)`.

**These counts are PER PASS.** Independent confirmation, not inference: the isometric draws all eight passes
and numbers each pass's radiant tubes **1 → 31 individually** (Passes 1, 2, 7 and 8 read directly off the
zoomed quadrants). A heater-wide count of 31 radiant tubes cannot give one pass 31 numbered tubes. The
convection bundles are drawn per pass in the "Passes" legend and are consistent with 16 tubes each, though
the scan will not resolve a clean count — the title-block value governs there.

**Convection.** 16 tubes × 65.0 ft = **1,040 ft per pass**; ×8 passes = **8,320 ft**. The CND24002 drawing's
"(16) 6" Sch 40 tubes, 63'-6" effective" is this same per-pass figure (63'-6" effective vs 65.0 ft overall).

**Radiant.** 31 tubes × 38.6 ft = **1,197 ft per pass**; ×8 passes = **9,573 ft**. Vertical serpentine;
the coil revamp sketch dimensions the legs at 38'-10" through 44'-7¾". Heaviest fouling is in the last
radiant tubes at the outlet — 29, 30, 31 (CND24002).

**Materials split within each coil** (Syncrude/CoSyn coil revamp BOM `7 1 D-F-015` Rev 1, read from a zoomed
crop of `F-1 DRAWINGS.pdf` p2): T-1 = 165 ft/coil of 6.625" × 0.280" seamless in **SA335-P5**, T-3 = 185
ft/coil in **SB423 (Incoloy 825)**; U-bends SA234-WP5 and SB425. Both totals are exactly 8× the per-coil
figure (1,320 ft and 1,480 ft), which independently confirms **8 coils**. Note this BOM is a *revamp* scope
covering ~350 ft per coil, not the full coil — it establishes material distribution and coil count, and must
not be read as coil length.
