### Estimate observations (recorded for the next bid here — not corrections to a submitted quote)

**Pigging hours look conservative against the benchmark.** Each coil is ≈2,200 ft. At the 100
ft/hr benchmark that is 22 hrs per coil, which is exactly the 22 hrs bid per 3-coil set — so the
bid priced a triple-mode parallel set at the bare single-coil rate with **no parallel-friction
allowance stated**. Against the routine multi-pass rows in the actuals rollup (47 and 52 ft/hr
per pig at modes 3 and 5), 22 hrs per set is optimistic. Whether that was deliberate pricing
pressure through Steady-Flux or an unexamined default is not recorded.

**Rig-in tier rests on no job walk.** 6 hrs is the Moderate tier. Both launcher elevations are
TBD and the run distance from the pumper to the connections is unknown, so the tier was inferred
rather than settled. If the radiant-outlet launchers are in the air, this is a Large (8 hr) job
or worse, and the pipefitter adder (+2 hrs, PBF supplies the fitters) would stack on top.
Rig-out mirrors rig-in at 6.

**Shift-boundary fragility.** 70 hrs is 5.83 shifts against 6 billed. Only 2 hrs of slack before
a 7th shift — a single rig-in tier miss or one filter-press capacity stall consumes it.

**Filter press capacity.** One press against one Trimax at triple mode. Not the known 2×-Trimax
failure mode, but 3 coils running at once on a first-time clean with no history is real solids
load. No stand-by was quoted (0 hrs) and press capacity is not named as a risk anywhere in the
proposal.

**Timeline inconsistency in the source.** The proposal and filename say "Spring 2026 planned
outage" while the schedule runs 7/21–7/24/2026. The bid's own disclaimer calls the timeline
illustrative, so this is a stale-template artifact rather than a scope conflict — but confirm
the real outage window before reusing these dates.

**Crew truck count contradiction.** The execution plan and the mob description both say (2)
crew trucks; the equipment billing block carries 1 crew truck at 70 hrs, and the mob build-up
carries 0 crew-truck miles. See the facility card.
