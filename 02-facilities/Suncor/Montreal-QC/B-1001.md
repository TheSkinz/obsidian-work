**Tube geometry notes.**

**Convection (BOM items 1A–9D, remarked "process...inlet" through "conv outlet").** 36 tubes total, ~1,859 ft

**Radiant (BOM items 10A–29D, remarked "radiant inlet" onward).** 80 tubes total, ~3,954 ft

Total: 116 tubes, ~5,813 ft heater-total (both decoke circuits combined). 116 ÷ 4 passes = 29 tubes/pass, matching the elevation sketch's row numbering (1–29) — good cross-check between the two source drawings.

Fittings BOM confirms 4" as the coil's working size (return bends F1/F2 qty 32+74, elbows F3/F4, all A234-WP9), with size-up transitions toward the outlet manifold: 6"×4" reducers (qty 4), 10"×6" reducers (qty 2), 6" tee (qty 2), 10" tee (qty 1) — this matches the 10"-300# manifold line already on file (obturated for decoke per work order AB-52). The 2" fittings (F16–F22, flanges/blinds/elbolets) are very likely instrument or vent branch connections, not part of the pigging path — not counted in the tube tally above.

Process coil design data (from coil data block): Inlet 311°F @ 142 psig, Outlet 630°F @ 42.4 psig (operating). Design: pressure 450 psig, fluid temp 760°F, tube wall temp 935°F, corrosion allowance 0.125", shop hydrotest 1125 psig.

**Circuit split confirmed from the field, 2026-09-17 (Jesse), after CAD26002 ran this heater: the card's A+D / B+C pairing is correct.** DI-GE-5-007 §.1 describes the split as central convection + upper radiant against outer convection + lower radiant, which reads as a different membership; it is not. That language describes section elevation, not which passes share a decoke circuit, and it should not be re-raised as a contradiction. *(This was contradiction #2 of four in the 2026-09-08 RFQ-vs-cards note. B-102 resolved the same way; B-101 resolved the other way and its card was rebuilt.)*

Launcher/receiver access at grade is worth carrying into any rig-in figure for this heater: connections 5 ft up are the low-elevation case, so the 6-hr default governs and neither the 8-hr nor the 12-hr departure is in play.

Temporary spool dimensions (page 14 BOM): 4 straight spools at 4'-0" to 5'-0" long (Mk1–Mk6 family), pipe spec UD1 (water/soda ash), design 700 psig/370°F, hydrotest 1050 psig — same spec family as B-101 and B-102 temp spools. **UD1 is a spool material rating, not a process instruction: this heater takes no soda ash** (DI-GE-5-007 §.6 prints `N/A — NO SS IN TUBES` against B-1001). Reading UD1 as soda-ash scope is exactly the over-generalization corrected on 2026-09-08 — see [[02-facilities/Suncor/Montreal-QC/_facility]].

## Field Notes

### RFQ Intake — 2026-07

**Metallurgy contradiction with the site directive — ruled 2026-09-17 (Jesse): the BOM governs, this card stands.** DI-GE-5-007 §.6 splits this heater's metallurgy as convection `SA213 T5 (5CR-1/2MO)` and radiant `SA213 T9 (9CR-1MO)`, against the uniform SA213 9Cr-1Mo recorded here. **The tube/pipe BOM is the fabrication document and lists every tube item as SA213**, while §.6 is a summary table inside a standing site directive dated 2020-01-10 that was reused for this turnaround. Recorded rather than silently dropped so the next reader does not re-open it. Note the residual risk honestly: neither document is dated against the other, so a partial re-tube between them would make both right about different eras, and nobody has asked Suncor. Metallurgy is not observable from the field, so CAD26002 could not settle it.

**Obstacles (resolved 2026-07-07):** A clean tube/pipe BOM snippet closed the tube-count gap — 116 tubes total (36 convection + 80 radiant), 4.5"×0.207" wall SA213 (9Cr-1Mo), 5,813 ft heater-total. Two decoke circuits (not a single circuit) remains the key estimating driver — duration math must account for 2 separate rig-ins/pig runs, not one. Per-circuit (as opposed to per-pass) split of convection/radiant footage is an even-split assumption, not independently confirmed from the BOM.

**Facility Procedures:** Work order 60093250 (ARRÊT 2021) requires: (1) obturation for decoke, (2) install decoke equipment, (3) remove all instrumentation on the decoke circuit, (4) proceed with decoke — instrumentation removal is an explicit pre-step here, more so than on the other four heaters in this package. Doubled thermocouples on this circuit (TE-802-1 through -10) per P&ID notes.
