**Tube geometry notes.**

**Convection.** The tube schedule lists tubes 1–10 per pass (flow A & B): tube 1 at 39'-4", tubes 2 through 9 at 35'-6", tube 10 at 39'-4". Tubes 1–2 carry 1" carbon-steel fins, 3–4 1" 11% Cr fins, 5–6 ¾" 11% Cr fins, 7–10 bare. Wall is given as "Sch 120 avg. wall" with no figure; ID and lengths per circuit are left blank because the drawing states neither.

**Radiant (segment 1 of 3).** Tube 11 at 39'-4" and tubes 12 through 25 at 36'-3¾" (28 required for both passes), 4½" OD.

**Radiant (segment 2 of 3).** Tubes 26 (35'-7¼") and 27 (36'-0¾"), 6⅝" OD Sch 80. Joined to segment 1 through a 4×6 concentric reducer, Sch 120 (fittings list).

**Radiant (segment 3 of 3).** Tube 28 at 38'-10", 8⅝" OD Sch 80, the outlet tube. A 6×8 concentric reducer, Sch 80, is in the fittings list.

**Return bends.** The fittings list carries 32 × 4" IPS 180° cast U-bends Sch 120 (8" c/c, short radius); 14 × 4" 850# series mule-ear welded U-bends (8" c/c, Sch 120); 2 × 4" 850# mule ears (12" c/c); 2 × 6" 850# mule ears (16" c/c, Sch 80); 2 × 6" IPS 180° U-bends Sch 80. Which tubes carry cast bends and which carry mule ears is not stated. An external crossover joins convection tube 10 to radiant tube 11.
