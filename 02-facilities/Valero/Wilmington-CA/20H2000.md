**Tube geometry notes.**

**Convection.** The GA (WE-20-0202-H) calls out 4 rows (8 tubes) of 4.5" OD × 0.237" AW pipe finned 1.0" × 0.05" at 5 CS fins/inch, 1 row (2) finned 0.75" × 0.05" at 3 11% Cr fins/inch, and 2 rows (4) bare shield tubes — 14 tubes, two per row. Effective tube length 23'-3". Two "future rows" are drawn above. The drawing does not state the pass count or tubes per pass.

**Radiant (segment 1 of 2).** GA callout "(29) 4.500 OD × 0.237 AW pipe, matl ASTM A335 P9 (9Cr-1Mo)", drawn on the wall; effective tube length 23'-0". Whether 29 is per wall or heater total is not stated.

**Radiant (segment 2 of 2).** "(One) 6.625 OD × 0.280 AW pipe, matl ASTM A312 TP316L" at the outlet terminal, el. ~3'-0". A revision cloud on the lower radiant reads "Revise crude increase, see dwgs WD-20-0240-H thru WD-20-0242-H" — those sheets were not supplied.

**Return bends.** Jesse's reading of the GA longitudinal section (WE-20-0202-H), 2026-10-08: the **radiant is plug-header on one end and U-bends on the other**, and the **convection looks to be all U-bends**. The plugs sit at the end opposite the convection inlet flange (the right side as drawn, where the GA marks "header box"). The inlet-flange end carries U-bends. The coil drawing should confirm which tubes are on plugs.

**Missing.** The coil drawing (pressure-parts sheet) (79-614-5-T-11, "Pressure Parts – Conv, X-Overs & Rad, to include tube & fitting descriptions, coil fabrication notes & details") is listed on the package's own drawing index but is not in it. Everything blank above is waiting on that sheet.
