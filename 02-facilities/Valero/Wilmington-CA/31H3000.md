**Tube geometry notes.**

**Convection.** The 2017 crossover iso (31H3000-7) draws the convection bank feeding two crossovers, one to radiant roof section B and one to roof section E. The 1973 GA notes a later tube-bundle addition and U-bend relocation ("see WD-31-100-1", not supplied). The GA scan is too low-resolution to read tube callouts.

**Radiant.** The iso labels six radiant sections: B (roof) → A (wall) → diagonal crossover → C (center) on one side, and E → D → F mirrored. It references section sheets 1–6 and convection sheet 8; none were supplied. GA states "31'-2" effective tube length" and 32'-8" out-to-out casing. Lower C and F tubes are drawn at wider spacing than the upper ones; whether that marks a bore change is not stated.
