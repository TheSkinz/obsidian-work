## Scope

Mechanical decoke and third-party smart pig inspection of **100-H-1, 100-H-2 and H-1102** on one mobilization. **One Trimax pumper with filtration.** T&M on execution, mob/demob lump sum, payment terms 30 days, third-party at cost + 15%.

Triggered by an unscheduled callout: 100-H-1 leaked a tube on startup following a planned steam-air decoke (Valero Inspection RR `100-RR-008`, Pass #2 Radiant Tube #15, photographed 2026-08-21). The customer rep added 100-H-2 and the preflash reboiler to the same mobilization.

**Execution configuration.** Convection inlet flanges looped in pairs so four passes become two circuits per heater; all launchers and receivers on temporary 8" pig spools at the radiant outlet flanges, **at grade**; double mode. No elevated rigging in USADebusk's scope — the customer's pipefitters hang the inlet jumpers.
