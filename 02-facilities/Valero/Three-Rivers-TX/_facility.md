## Notes

Facility added 2026-08-22 during an emergency callout. **The site had no prior record in this vault** — a grep across all notes including `INDEX.md` returned zero matches on the site name, the ZIP and the street before this card was created.

**The OneDrive estate holds a `Valero Three Rivers TX\_History\` folder containing 2008–2011 worksheets and quotations under the Eveready and Clean Harbors predecessor brands. Jesse ruled these out of scope for current work and they were not opened.** That exclusion covers USADebusk's own predecessor-brand job files. It does **not** cover the customer's OEM drawings: the 1977 RADCO general arrangements for the preflash reboiler were supplied by the customer rep and are in scope, because original equipment drawings do not expire the way old quotes do.

**Callout context (2026-08-21/22).** Valero Inspection issued RR `100-RR-008` after 100-H-1 leaked a tube on startup following a planned steam-air decoke — Pass #2, Radiant Tube #15, photographed 2026-08-21. Repair is cut-and-replace with a P9 procedure, PWHT 1325–1400 °F, BHN 179–241, hydrotest to 150 psi. The customer rep subsequently added 100-H-2 and the preflash reboiler to the same mobilization. Jesse walked the job 2026-08-22.

**Execution configuration set at the job walk** (applies to all three heaters): convection inlet flanges looped in pairs so four passes become two circuits; launchers and receivers on temporary 8" pig spools at the radiant outlet flanges, which are at grade; double mode. This removes all elevated rigging from USADebusk's scope — the only work at height is the customer's pipefitters hanging the inlet jumpers.

**Vendor.** SteadyFlux supplied a drawing-analysis table and an intelligent-pigging quotation of $262,600 covering all three heaters. Their tube counts, IDs, ODs, wall thicknesses and metallurgy check out against the drawings; **their footage column does not** and should not be used — it is a uniform-length × tube-count approximation that overstates 100-H-1 by 21%. Detail on each heater card.
