## Notes

**Verification.** Built 2026-08-22 from primary sources supplied by the customer rep. **Stated outright** by those sources: tube count 172, tube lengths 39.5 ft and 20.5 ft, both tube sizes with their IDs and walls, metallurgy 9Cr-1Mo, crude service, four passes, and the per-pass tube numbering that yields 22 convection and 21 radiant. **Derived** here and not stated anywhere: every footage figure in the tables above, which is tube count × stated length. **Not verified at all:** launcher flange rating, water source, return bend type in the convection, the post-2019 count of 8" radiant tubes, and the pumper-to-flange run distance.

**Source vintage.** The Quest Integrity FTIS coil map is dated 09/26/2011 (drawing `TR-W2388/09-11`) and is described in RR `100-RR-008` as the last smart pig report — so the inspection baseline on this heater is fifteen years old and predates the 2019 retube below. Tube counts survive a like-for-like hairpin replacement; the **diameter split does not**, which is the one figure on this card most likely to be stale.

**2019 retube (GST job CN-18-708).** Great Southern Technologies fabricated replacement radiant hairpins in 6" Sch 40 A-335-P9 to displace existing 8" tubes and returns, mating back to the existing 8" outlet piping through an 8×6 concentric reducer and a 2'-0" 8" Sch 40 stub, one per pass. Drawing `03-01` covers four hairpins — eight tubes — against 28 tubes at 7.981" present in 2011. Either further `03-xx` fabrication sheets exist or the conversion was partial; **the sheets read do not settle how many 8" tubes remain in service.** Companion demo drawing `10-01` was referenced but not supplied.

**Tube leak, 2026-08-21.** Valero Inspection RR `100-RR-008`: a tube leaked on startup after a planned steam-air decoke, located through the southwest peep door at **Pass #2, Radiant Tube #15** — the first tube of the 8" centre row. Valero's repair scope is cut out the section, PMI to confirm A335 P9, preheat 400 °F, weld to a P5B-P5B procedure, PWHT 1325–1400 °F, BHN 179–241, and hydrotest to 150 psi to check for further leaks. Valero also recommends a floor inspection, tube strapping, and UT of all CMLs once the damage mechanism is defined. That is Valero's inspection scope, not USADebusk's.

**SteadyFlux drawing analysis — do not use their footage.** Their table gives 100-H-1 as 3,360 ft radiant and 2,860 ft convection, 6,220 ft total, against the 5,122 ft derived above — **21% high**. Their radiant is close (84 tubes is correct, and the 56/28 split at 6.065/7.981 matches the coil map exactly; they used 40.0 ft where the map says 39.5). Their convection is wrong twice: **110 tubes against an actual 88**, exactly one extra pass's worth, and **26.0 ft per tube against an actual 20.5**. The arithmetic gives the method away — every one of their figures is a round uniform length times a tube count.

**DSP#26100 quoted basis** (not an actual — quotation sent 2026-08-22). **Rig-in 6 · Pig 8 · Smart pig 6 · Rig-out 6 = 26 hrs.** Two circuits at 2,561 ft each, double mode, 1 Trimax. The pigging line is a **task allowance, not footage ÷ rate** — the coil had just been steam-air decoked, so the 8 hrs buys fill, flush, one run of the size progression and polishing rather than removing a coke load. At 2,561 ft that implies ~320 ft/hr, which is why no ft/hr reasoning applies here. Rig-in is 6 as the **first heater on the mobilization**; 100-H-2 and H-1102 take 4 because the pumper is already partially rigged. Equipment $18,360 · labor $4,586.04 · materials $3,515.40 · per diem $1,350 · SteadyFlux $106,260 (cost + 15%) — heater total **$134,071.44**.
