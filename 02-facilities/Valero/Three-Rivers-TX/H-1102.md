## Notes

**Verification.** Built 2026-08-22 from the RADCO general arrangements supplied by the customer rep. **Stated outright:** every tube count, every tube length, tube size and schedule, A-106B metallurgy, short-radius 180° weld return bends, the 4" 300# RFWN connections with Sch 80 bore, four passes A/B/C/D, and the full coil data block. **Derived here:** the average lengths and all footage totals, computed by extending the schedule's own per-row counts and lengths. Both sections' tube counts cross-check against the schedule's independently stated pipe quantities, which is why this card carries a verified date despite the drawing's age. **Not verified:** water source, whether the coil is still as-drawn, and the tag ambiguity in the warning above.

**Drawing vintage — the real caveat.** RADCO job 144 was drawn in **1977** for **Sigmor Refining Co.**, a predecessor owner of the Three Rivers refinery. An original OEM general arrangement does not go stale the way an old quote does, so this is legitimately usable — but a 49-year-old coil may have been retubed or modified, and nothing supplied says it has not. **Confirm the coil is as-drawn before this footage governs a quoted duration.** This is distinct from the vault's rule excluding pre-2012 predecessor-*contractor* files from the OneDrive `_History` folder: that rule is about USADebusk's own old job paperwork, not the customer's equipment drawings.

**Condition, 2026-08.** Jesse confirmed this heater was **not** part of the recent steam-air decoke that preceded 100-H-1's tube leak — normal service fouling, so 100-H-1's light-clean rate basis does not transfer here.

**SteadyFlux drawing analysis — counts good, convection footage wrong.** Their table gives 68 radiant tubes and 64 convection, both exactly right, with correct ID, OD, wall and A-106B metallurgy. Their radiant footage of 2,686 ft is 68 × 39.5, which misses the eight longer inlet and outlet tubes — 22 ft low, negligible. **Their convection footage of 1,024 ft is 64 × 16.0, and no 16-ft tube appears anywhere in the schedule** — 182 ft high against the stated rows, 22%. Same uniform-length method that overstated 100-H-1.

**DSP#26100 quoted basis** (not an actual — quotation sent 2026-08-22). **Rig-in 4 · Pig 18 · Smart pig 4 · Rig-out 4 = 30 hrs.** Two circuits at 1,775 ft each, double mode, 1 Trimax. Pigging is `1,775 ÷ 100 = 17.75 → 18` — the **bare benchmark, nearest even hour, no derate**, despite preflash service and a first-time clean. Rig-in 4 as a subsequent heater. A **facility-rented 4×3 pump** bills 30 hrs at $80/hr ($2,400) and is not a USADebusk mobilized piece, so it carries no mob/demob mileage. Equipment $24,900 · labor $5,288.76 · materials $1,657 (20 × 4" pins + DEF) · per diem $1,230 · SteadyFlux $89,470 — heater total **$122,545.76**.
