**Tube geometry notes.**

**Convection.** Not in the EJP package. THI's 2007 job (SJ866) replaced the radiant section and transition only; the GA labels the convection "EXISTING CONVECTION SECTION", 16'-2" tall, inlet centreline EL 172'-1¼". Metallurgy is the P&ID data block's "347 SS". Arrangement is the schema default, not stated.

**Radiant — segment 1 of 2.** SJ866-4A tube BOM marks 12A–19D: 32 × 8" Sch 40, 44'-6", A312-TP347H, so 8 per pass. Arrangement read from the plan view and the P&ID's "vertical cylindrical type". ID is left blank by rule; Sch 40 computes to 7.981".

**Radiant — segment 2 of 2.** Marks 20A–20D (4 × 6" Sch 40, 44'-0") and 21A–21D (4 × 6" Sch 40, 43'-7½"), one of each per pass; average 43.8 ft. Two 8×6 concentric reducers per pass (F2, qty 8) step down at tube 20 and back up to 8" after tube 21 into the outlet flange. ID computes to 6.065" for Sch 40.
