## Scope Summary
Mechanical decoking of the 27GF1A F-201 Treat Gas heater with Smart Pig support. One Trimax
Pumper, one support unit, filtration. Four coils looped to two circuits via temporary 5"
loops at the convection inlets; all four passes decoked in conjunction, then smart-pig
inspected. All pigs are launched and received at the radiant outlets through 5"×6" 300#
adapters the customer welds on. Treat Gas scope only — the superheat steam service in the
same heater is excluded.

- **Basis:** Time & Material. Mob/Demob lump sum.
- **Equipment:** 1 Trimax Pumper · 1 Support Unit · 1 Filtration Unit · 2 Crew Trucks
- **Duration:** 36 hrs total (6 Rig-in | 18 Pig | 6 Smart Pig | 6 Rig-out) · 1.5 days · 3 shifts · 108 man-hours
- **Window:** 2027-01-18 07:00 → 2027-01-19 19:00
- **Crew plan:** 1 Day Supervisor + 2 Day Operators / 1 Night Supervisor + 2 Night Operators (6 total)
- **Prepared by:** Jason Harman
- **Project Manager (quoted):** Travis Trenholm — not billed as a line item on this quote
- **Bid due:** 2026-07-01 · **Valid through:** 2026-09-29

## Notes

**Quoted against an open radiant section.** No radiant coil drawings were available at bid
time — radiant tube quantity, pass length, tube ID and metallurgy are all unrecorded on
[[F-201]]. The 18 pigging hours therefore rest on convection footage plus working assumptions
about radiant leg length that the workup itself grades Low confidence. This is the single
largest exposure on the quote and should be revisited before award or before any revision.

**Stale quantity in the workup's billing-summary block.** The per-heater summary block reads
`15 × 5" Pin`, while the materials revenue block reads `20 × 5" Pin` and the $2,070.00 total
resolves to 20 (20 × $103.50). **20 is the figure that reached the customer.** The 15 is a
stale cell that does not feed the total. Worth correcting in the sheet so a future reader does
not price off it.

**Crew truck: 2 allocated, 1 billed.** The Equipment and Manpower Allocation lists 2 Crew
Trucks, and the mob build-up carries 2 crew trucks at 0 miles. The equipment revenue block
bills one crew truck at 36 hrs. At $25/hr the swing is $900 — small, but the enumerated count
and the billed count disagree and neither was flagged at bid time.

originally carried `DSP#:26000` — a stale metafile paste traced to an unrelated placeholder
draft. Patched to `26085` on 2026-07-25 with Jesse's go-ahead; the total held at $40,477.08
and the corrected file was promoted in the Bids folder. This version was never in
