## Notes

**Pre-job budget estimate (DSP25123 Rev 2, not actuals):** 33 project hours across 2 days / 3 shifts — 6 rig-in · 12 pig · 9 smart pig · 0 rig-over · 0 stand-by · 6 rig-out. 132 direct labor hours. Crew: 1 day supervisor + 3 day operators, 1 night supervisor + 3 night operators. Equipment: 1 TriMax, 1 support unit, 2 crew trucks. PM Travis Trenholm. Quoted total $39,905.41.

**⚠ Job number shared with F-802.** Invoice 1018569 records `Job Number: USA# 26022` and a service period of `4.28 - 5.7.2026` — the same job number and the same dates already carried on [[F-802]] for its own decoke. F-901 nonetheless ran on a separate ExxonMobil PO (4510719347) with its own invoice, and Jesse's account is that it ran *after* F-802 finished. Two readings are open: either USADeBusk booked both heaters under one job number and the invoice's date field is the F-802 span copied forward, or the F-901 execution dates genuinely fall inside that window. This matters because the F-802 card's Task Durations row is keyed `USA26022` — if F-901 shares the number, that key is no longer unique per heater. Related open thread: the F-802 card flags an unexplained `USA26031` appearing on the USA26022 service receipts; whether that is the F-901 job is unresolved. **Needs Jesse.**

**⚠ Max pig OD conflict — SOP is wrong.** The governing ID is 5.043" (process and treat gas coils), which caps the final pig at 5.043 + 0.250 = 5.293", rounding down to the 5.25" pig. SOP-DCK-F901-001 REV 0 states 5.293" correctly in its Operating Parameters table but then instructs in Phase II to "continue upsizing until maximum pig OD is reached: 5.5" for Pass 1 and Pass 2; 5.5" for Pass 3" — and the F-901 diagram repeats 5.5". A 5.5" pig is 0.457" over a 5.043" ID and 0.453" over the 5.047" steam coil. The SOP contradicts itself, and the wrong number is the one in the executable procedure. The card carries 5.25" per the sizing rule. **This is a customer-issued document — needs correction before F-901 is worked again.**

**⚠ Filtration conflict.** DSP25123 Technical Data records `Filtration: No` on all four sections and the quote prices no filtration. The SOP and diagram both specify a DeBusk filter press with a full recycle loop (dirty tank → 4×3 pump → press → clean tank), and the SOP's rig-out steps call for removing it. Job Options records the *quoted* status (Declined); whether a press actually went out with the job is unresolved and can't be settled from the fixed-price invoice.

**⚠ Radiant footage does not reconcile.** The quote states 18 tubes/circuit at approximately 89 ft tube+bend, which multiplies to 1,602 ft — but states 1,200 ft per circuit and 2,400 ft total. 1,200 ÷ 18 gives 66.7 ft/tube. Every other section multiplies out exactly (Conv 24×16=384, Treat Gas 56×16=896, Steam 8×17=136), so the radiant "~89 ft" is the suspect figure. The card carries the stated 1,200 / 2,400 footage, which is what the SOP and diagram also use for the 3,168 ft Pass 1 circuit. Do not use ~89 ft for anything without confirming against a coil drawing.

**Quote-number discrepancy.** Both Rev 1 and Rev 2 PDFs carry `Quotation # DSP#: 25084 Rev N` in the quotation cell, and ExxonMobil's PO cites `QUOTATION # DSP#: 25084 R`. DSP25084 is the F-802 quote — the F-901 workbook was built from the F-802 template and the number cell was never updated. The filename on both revisions reads DSP# 25123, and a quote number cannot address two different heaters, so **25123 is the number of record**; 25084 in the body is a template artifact. Recorded on [[DSP25123]].

**Boilerplate defect in the issued quote.** Section 3 of the Time & Material Rate Charges page names *Citgo* three times as the party responsible for waste containers, wash area, and waste manifesting — in a document issued to ExxonMobil. Present in both Rev 1 and Rev 2, so it went to the customer. Worth fixing in whatever template that page comes from.
