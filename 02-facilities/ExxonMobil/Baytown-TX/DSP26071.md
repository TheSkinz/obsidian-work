## Scope Summary
Mechanical decoking of the HU5A F-501 heater — all four process coils (convection + radiant)
plus the Treat Gas section — with Smart Pig assist. One TriMax Pumper in triple-pump mode,
one support unit, DeBusk filter press with 4×3 trash pump. Four process passes looped to two
continuous circuits via temporary 5" 300# 180° spools at the convection inlets; Treat Gas runs
as a standalone third circuit.

- **Basis:** Time & Material. Mob/Demob lump sum.

- **Duration:** 48 hrs total (8 Rig-in | 24 Pig | 8 Smart Pig | 8 Rig-out) · 2 days · 4 shifts · 144 man-hours

quotation and the workbook Execution sheet show `8/10/27 07:00 → 8/12/27 07:00` while every
header on the same documents says "August 2026 TA." The turnaround is August 2026 — equipment

**Convection flange size disagrees across our own documents.** The workbook Heater Data sheet
says 6" 300# for the convection inlet/outlet; the quotation's Technical Data table and the
SOP both say 5" 300# RFWN. The SOP and diagram govern (5" 300# RFWN, with the 5"×6" reducing
spools appearing only at the radiant outlets). The quotation's table is the one that is wrong.

**Workbook Heater Data still reads "4: Looped to 1 Pass" for convection** — stale from Rev 1.
Rev 2, the SOP, and the diagram all specify 4 process passes looped to 2 circuits.
