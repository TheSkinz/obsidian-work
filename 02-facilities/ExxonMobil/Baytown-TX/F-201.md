## Notes

**Radiant data gap is the dominant uncertainty on this card.** The workup's own confidence
grades say so plainly: radiant can dimensions High, convection tube OD/ID High, Treat Gas
BOM tube counts High for visible rows, but **full developed radiant inlet/outlet length Low**
and **complete circuit footage Medium-Low until pass routing is resolved.** The radiant tube
lengths carried in the workup as `[WORKING] 32'-0"` for intermediate, inlet-visible-leg and
outlet-visible-leg are working assumptions off an estimated 31'–32.5" visible leg, not stated
facts, and the workup flags that developed lengths run longer than visible straight legs
because crossover and return geometry were not available. None of those working figures are
carried onto this card.

**Radiant can geometry (drawing anchors, High confidence).** Radiant can height 11,000 mm
(36'-1.1") · can O.D. reference 6,420 mm (21'-0.8") · refractory I.D. 6,002 mm (19'-8.3") ·
tube circle diameter 5,622 mm (18'-5.3") · burner circle diameter 2,000 mm (6'-6.7") ·
radiant inlet elevation EL. 12,251 mm (40'-2.3") · B.O. floor elevation EL. 1,000 mm
(3'-3.4"). These are can dimensions, not coil dimensions — they do not close the footage gap.

**Scope boundary — Treat Gas only.** The workup marks the scope `[FINAL] Heater = Birwelco
USA Inc. / F-201 / Treat Gas scope only` and `[FINAL] Steam Scope = Excluded; SH Steam rows
107-108 disregarded`. The superheat steam service in the same heater is out of scope.

**Launcher elevation is not recorded and the rig tier was never grounded in a job walk.**
The radiant inlet sits at EL. 40'-2.3", which puts the radiant section high, but the outlet
connection elevation — the number that actually sets the rig-in tier — is not in the source.
DSP26085 priced 6 hrs rig-in / 6 hrs rig-out. If this heater goes to award, settle the
launcher/receiver elevation and the pumper-to-connection run distance at the job walk before
trusting that tier.
