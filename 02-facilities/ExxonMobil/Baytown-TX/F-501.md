### Source discrepancies carried forward

**Radiant tubes per pass.** The workbook says 60 total / 15 per pass; the SOP's spec table says
"60 total / 30 per pass." The SOP's 30 is per *circuit* (two passes looped), not per pass. The
card carries 15/pass, which is what divides cleanly into 4 passes.

**Footage.** Convection 680 ft (SOP) vs 678 ft (workbook); radiant 2,472 ft (SOP) vs 2,475 ft
(workbook). The card carries the SOP figures, which are internally consistent with the per-pass
lengths (170 × 4 = 680; 618 × 4 = 2,472).

**Convection flange.** Workbook Heater Data says 6" 300#; the quotation's Technical Data table
and the SOP say 5" 300# RFWN. The card carries 5" 300# RFWN per the SOP and diagram — the 5"×6"
reducers appear only at the radiant outlets, which is consistent only with a 5" convection flange.

**SOP REV 1 carries stale sequential-execution language — no reissue required.** REV 1 states on
page 1 that the three circuits "are operated sequentially, one circuit at a time," and Phase II
is written as "performed sequentially: Circuit 1, then Circuit 2, then Circuit 3." That text
survives from an earlier scope revision and is **wrong for the awarded scope**. All three coils
are cleaned **simultaneously in triple mode** — Circuit 1 on the left pump, Circuit 2 on center,
Circuit 3 on right, running concurrently, which is what the REV 1 circuit-mapping table and the
rig diagram already show. The pump assignments in REV 1 are correct; only the sequencing prose
is stale.

**This is not an open action.** ExxonMobil Baytown requires the SOP at bid stage only — it gave
their engineers what they needed and does not get reissued after award (see [[_facility]], Site
Safety and Procedures). REV 1 stands as the bid artifact. **This card and the job sheet are the
execution authority for the configuration**, not the SOP PDF. Corrected by Jesse 2026-07-27.
