## Scope Summary
Mechanical decoking and Smart Pigging support of the PS8 F-901 Kerosene Furnace at ExxonMobil Baytown, using a single TriMax Pumper in Triple Mode. Four coils are executed across three pigging circuits: the two process coils (Convection + Radiant) are looped at the radiant outlets into one combined circuit, the Treat Gas coil runs as a single pass, and the Superheat Steam coil runs as a single pass. All three are pigged simultaneously.

- **Basis:** Time & Material. Mob/Demob quoted, not waived ($2,784 each way).
- **Equipment:** (1) TriMax Pumper · (1) Support Unit · (2) Crew Trucks · Launchers & Receivers
- **Duration:** 33 project hours (6 rig-in | 12 pig | 9 smart pig | 6 rig-out) · 2 days · 3 shifts · 132 direct labor hrs
- **Manpower:** 1 day supervisor · 3 day operators · 1 night supervisor · 3 night operators
- **PM:** Travis Trenholm · **Prepared by:** Jason Harman (jharman@usadebusk.com · 713-839-5961)
- **Smart pigging:** Steady-Flux, ExxonMobil's vendor of choice. USADeBusk provides water propulsion only.
- **Composite rate for delays/overages:** $985.98/hr inclusive of material, equipment, and labor.

## Quote-Number Discrepancy

Both revisions carry `Quotation # DSP#: 25084 Rev N` in the quotation header cell, and ExxonMobil's PO cites `QUOTATION # DSP#: 25084 R`. DSP25084 is the **F-802** quote — the F-901 workbook was built from the F-802 template and the number cell was never updated. The filename on both revision PDFs reads DSP# 25123, and one quote number cannot address two different heaters at two different values, so **25123 is the number of record**. The consequence to be aware of: ExxonMobil's PO and their AP records point at 25084, so a records request against 25084 will return two unrelated scopes.
