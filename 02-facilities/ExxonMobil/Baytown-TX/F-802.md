**Tube geometry notes.**

**Radiant — corrected 2026-09-17 (Jesse).** The card was a faithful transcription of `SOP-DCK-F802-001 REV 0`, which assumed **6" Sch 80 pipe**. ExxonMobil's own F-802 inspection report — taken immediately before the April/May 2026 convection replacement, confirmed by Jesse 2026-09-09 — shows the heater runs **ASTM A213 tube, sized by OD and wall rather than by pipe schedule**. The radiant was never replaced, so that report describes the section as it stands today. Four values corrected: Tubes/Circuit `30 → 31`, Wall `0.432 → 0.500`, ID `5.761 → 5.625` (computed, 6.625 − 2 × 0.500), Metallurgy `A335 Gr P9 → ASTM A213 T9`. Corrected geometry for the original coil also sits on [[F-801]] and agrees.

**Length/Circuit is deliberately NOT changed** and remains the SOP's 1,200 ft. The inspection report states no radiant tube length, so a recomputed `31 × ~39` would be a derived figure replacing a stated one — and F-802's footage feeds the **52 ft/hr row at `04-knowledge/estimating-actuals-rollup.md:23`**, one of only five routine rows in the ft/hr baseline. That row stays frozen until a stated tube length turns up.

**Convection — unverified, not proven wrong, and untouched.** That section *was* replaced in April/May 2026, so the inspection report's convection rows describe steel no longer in the heater. The card's 5.761" may well be right for the replacement. **The replacement section's spec sheet has been outstanding since June 2026** and the asterisked "verify against replacement spec" has never been cleared — nobody has ever verified what is currently in F-802's convection. The card also carries a known footage defect here (12 tubes × ~39 ft = 468 ft, printed three lines above the SOP's "228 ft per pass"); it is **not** to be corrected, for the same rollup reason.

*Convection section replaced April/May 2026 immediately following USA26022 decoke. Tube ID recorded as 5.761" (6" Sch 80); wall derived from OD/ID — verify against replacement section spec sheet.

## Field Notes

### USA26022 — May 2026

**De-inventory and the diesel flush that was never run** (Jesse, 2026-09-26). Before execution Jesse found that the *previous* decoke had left a large amount of resid in the coils after de-inventory, so he told ExxonMobil he needed a thorough one. They said they did not know whether they could steam it out any better, and the project was prepped to combat the resid and pitch expected to be left over — which is why `SOP-DCK-F802-001` carries a diesel flush as an unconditional Phase I step. **In the event they steamed it for longer and achieved a thorough de-inventory, so the diesel flush was unnecessary and was not performed.** Nothing in the SOP or the durations schema records this, and the hours give no clue either way: there is no flush column, and rig-in ran 4 hrs against 12 quoted.

This is the precedent behind F-801's SOP writing the diesel circulation as a **conditional** Phase I-A rather than a standing step. Recorded because it existed only in Jesse's recollection, and the whole contingency framing rests on it.

**Pigs Ran:** (not recorded)

**Per-rig split (multi-Trimax only):** Trimax 4 (136 hrs total) and Trimax 6 (132 hrs total) — elapsed per-rig totals backing the Task Durations table above. One pumper ran triple mode, the other double mode; all 5 passes pigged in conjunction (Mode 5). Condition routine — planned decoke, no upset (per Jesse, 2026-07-22).
