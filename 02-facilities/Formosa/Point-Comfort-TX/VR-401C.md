## Notes

### Corrections applied to the DSP#26075 source data

**1 — Convection tube count (39 vs 40), partially resolved.** The proposal's technical data
table lists 39 convection tubes plus a separate jumper; the "Technical Parameter" block on the
same workbook sheet says 40 convection tubes. The gap reconciles: that block counts the 45-ft
crossover jumper as a 16th 6.625" convection tube (11 Sch 40 + 4 Sch 80 + jumper = 16). This
card counts the jumper once, as its own section. **39 convection tubes + 1 jumper is correct.**

**2 — The single 5.0" OD tube, unresolved.** The same parameter block splits the 4.5" run as
23 tubes at 4.500" OD plus one at 5.0" OD, and flags it "CRITICAL." The customer-facing table
shows a flat 24 at 4.500". This card carries 24 at 4.500" because that is what the footage math
balances against (972.65 ft, and the 3,213.07 ft total), but the claim is live and unverified.
It does not move the max pig OD — 4.026" still governs either way — but it is a real obstruction
risk mid-convection if a *larger* pig is being pushed through. Two things are wrong with the
claim on its face: 5.0" is not a standard pipe OD (nominal 5" pipe is 5.563" OD), and no wall
or schedule was given for it. **Verify against the as-built before pigging this heater.**

**3 — Section footage.** The parameter block's "~1,700 ft convection / ~1,600 ft radiant" are
rounded approximations. This card uses the exact tube-table figures: 1,576.40 ft convection
(1,621.40 with the jumper) and 1,591.67 ft radiant.

**4 — Metallurgy handling.** VR-401C tubes are TP-347H stainless in both sections, yet the bid
package's scope split (§1.0 / §2.0) never mentions soda ash passivation, chloride limits on the
supplied water, or who performs neutralization. Recorded here as a gap, not a fact.

### Bid-package defects (not heater facts — recorded so a future bid does not repeat them)

The 750 CFM compressor was double-assigned: §1.2 puts it in Formosa's scope for dewatering,
§2.1 says USADebusk mobilizes it, and the equipment list on the cover and in the workup carries
no compressor at all (it carries the 4x3 diesel pump instead). The workbook line item for the
compressor is priced at $0. Execution date also conflicts — the cover says July 17, 2026 while
the schedule runs 7/14 07:00 to 7/16 21:00. The title says "(2) VR-401C Furnace Decokes" but
the schedule, workup, and price cover one decoke of one heater with zero rig-over hours.
