**Tube geometry notes.**

**Convection.** 36 tubes, marks 1–18 in A/B pairs across 2 circuits. Thirty finned (4½″ OD,
solid helically wound fins, 60/ft × 0.05″ × ¾″ high — 11%Cr on marks 1–11, 304SS on marks
11A/12–15) and six bare (marks 16–18). Lengths 62′-2″ typical, with 65′-8″ and 64′-11″ at the
inlet and outlet tubes. Summed **≈2,250 ft**. Section B-B shows tubes **2A, 1A, 1B** across at
**6″ | 2″ | 8″** spacing with two flanged connections — a 3-wide bank on 8″ centres, two
circuits, matching [[RW-0014-214.9]] exactly. Section C-C shows the convection outlet
(marks 17A, 18A, 18B) tying into **existing crossovers**.

> **Unreconciled material conflict, same sheet.** Rev 10 clouded the Tube & Pipe BOM to change
> marks 1A–18B to **347H**. The Tube Finning BOM on that same sheet still lists **A335-P9** for
> the same marks — the revision did not propagate. 347H is recorded here because it is the
> revised value and it matches the RW-0014-214.9 convection (SA-312-TP347H), but the drawing
> contradicts itself and neither reading is independently confirmed. Resolve with the customer
> before any passivation or water-source commitment.

**Radiant (segment 1 of 2).** Marks 31–46 A/B — 32 tubes at 64′-2″, summing **2,053 ft**.

**Radiant (segment 2 of 2).** Marks 47–50 A/B — 8 tubes summing **517 ft**: 47A/B at 63′-3″;
48A (one tube built from a 31′-5″ and a 33′-5″ piece, *one of each makes one*, **internally
alonized**, at mark 4E-1 only); 48B/49A/49B at 64′-2″; 50A/50B at 66′-10″ to the hydrocarbon
outlet. The 4″-to-5″ transition is made by **4 off 5″ × 4″ Sch 80 concentric reducers**
(mark F5, 2 of them spares) — a mid-pass ID change inside the radiant, not at the crossover.

> **The radiant tube count is incomplete.** Marks **19–30** — 24 tubes — appear nowhere in the
> Tube & Pipe BOM or the Ship-Loose list. SJ384-4A covers "#2 Coker & #3 Heater modifications";
> those are existing tubes being retained and were never drawn on this sheet. The 40 tubes /
> 2,571 ft recorded above is a **floor for the radiant, not a total**. Do not extrapolate a full
> radiant footage, tube count, or duration from this card. Arrangement is stated as Horizontal

**Connection notes.** Whether the F7/F8 flanges are terminal connections or intermediate is
**not established** on this sheet — they are listed in the Fittings BOM and the ship-loose list
without a resolvable location callout at this resolution. Do not treat either row as a
confirmed launcher or receiver position until verified.

**Bend limit.** The Fittings BOM lists **60 off 4″ Sch 80 short-radius 180° return bends**
(mark F1) plus **22 more shipped loose** (mark F9), against only 4 long-radius 4″ (F6) and 7
long-radius 5″ (F3/F4). Short-radius returns dominate this coil. As on [[RW-0014-214.9]], pig
body length — not diameter — is the binding constraint. Short-body or flexible pigs only.

**Construction sequence** (field note on the sheet): "ALL HAIRPINS TO BE INSTALLED FROM RETURN
END w/ CLOSING WELDS @ TERMINAL END." Seventeen hairpins ship loose (marks 4D-1 … 4D-8).

## Notes

**Verification:** 2026-08-04 (drawing extraction only — source is a ~39 DPI raster; no field verification, no job history)


**Design criteria** (sheet, Design Criteria block). Elastic design pressure: **300 psig** at the
outlet for the 5″ tubes, **450 psig** for the 4″ tubes, **650 psig** for the convection section
tubes. Rupture design pressure: **105 psig** at the outlet for the 5″ tubes; the rest of the
rupture block is below the legibility floor of the raster.

**Hydro-test** (general note 5): shop and field hydro the **convection coil at 975 psig**, the
**radiant coil at 450 psig**. The 975 psig convection figure is identical to
[[RW-0014-214.9]]'s — a third independent corroboration that the two convection sections are the
same design.

**Field constraint from general note 1:** coil material must be protected from paint and zinc
contaminates, and **tubes shall not come in contact with galvanized metal at any time**. That
applies to temporary hardware and staging brought to the coil, not only to fabrication.

Other general notes: PMI inspection of coil material per spec 0129-100065; coil welds stress
relieved, 100% radiographed, 100% MT after stressing, Brinell on 100% of coil welds; welding and
NDE per specs EL-0129-865080 and EL-0129-829100-2. Sheet is to be worked with drawings "4P",
"4D" and "4E" — **none of which are in hand**. Those three sheets are where the missing radiant
marks 19–30 most likely live; request them if this heater goes to bid.
