## Framing decided in the audit (2026-09-23)
- **Triple pumper at LAR = redundancy, not speed.** Every Carson heater is 2-pass and H-101 is pigged in pairs, so a dual pumper could run the same schedule. The deck now presents the third pump as an on-board spare (no stoppage if a pump fails; can drive a smart pig). Don't claim the triple makes LAR faster.
- **Galveston Bay** has no named contact on the reference list ("available upon request"). Slide 22 now says named contacts at 8 sites.
- **Overrun answer:** the lump sum excludes additional fouling, unknown repairs and stoppages outside our control (proposal T&C 5). Say so if asked.
- **Case studies (slide 23):** Marathon Detroit 70H1 coker (Sept 2024 and Sept 2026) leads; CHS McPherson HF-0012 second. Phillips 66 was dropped in revision 7.
- **Detroit facts:** 6 passes (3 cells × 2), 6,372' convection + 8,784' radiant = 15,156', two TriMax units pigging all six passes at once (from the 70H1 technical data Jesse supplied). No hours are quoted. The slide says both were completed on schedule and safely (Jesse, 2026-09-24).
- **Pressure (confirmed by Jesse):** normal pigging 150–300 psi; pump rated 600 psi (Waterous curve, appendix slide 27); 500–550 psi is the realistic ceiling after losses through our valves. Don't call 600 a working limit.
- **Still worth confirming:** 3,000/2,000 gal tank sizes (equipment library); RFI item numbers SP-2 and GD-1.
