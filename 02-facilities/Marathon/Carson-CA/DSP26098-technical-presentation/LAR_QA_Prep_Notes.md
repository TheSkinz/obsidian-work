## Framing decided in the audit (2026-09-23)
- **Triple pumper at LAR = redundancy, not speed.** Every Carson heater is 2-pass and H-101 is pigged in pairs, so a dual pumper could run the same schedule. The deck now presents the third pump as an on-board spare (no stoppage if a pump fails; can drive a smart pig). Don't claim the triple makes LAR faster.
- **Galveston Bay** has no named contact on the reference list ("available upon request"). Slide 20 now says named contacts at 8 sites.
- **Overrun answer:** the lump sum excludes additional fouling, unknown repairs and stoppages outside our control (proposal T&C 5). Say so if asked.
- **Case studies (slide 21) are named:** CHS McPherson HF-0012 and Phillips 66 Ponca City H-28/H-29.
- **Case-study causes (slide 21):** the challenge and lesson wording comes from the heater cards. The planned-vs-emergency hours gap shouldn't be credited entirely to planning; fouling differs between the two jobs.
- **Pressure (confirmed by Jesse):** normal pigging 150–300 psi; pump rated 600 psi (Waterous curve, appendix slide 25); 500–550 psi is the realistic ceiling after losses through our valves. Don't call 600 a working limit.
- **Still worth confirming:** 3,000/2,000 gal tank sizes (equipment library); RFI item numbers SP-2 and GD-1.
