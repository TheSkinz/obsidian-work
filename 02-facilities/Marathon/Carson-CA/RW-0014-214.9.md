**Tube geometry notes.**

**Convection (process).** 36 tubes total across 2 passes, arranged **3 wide × 12 rows** on 8″
horizontal centers and 6 15/16″ vertical pitch. Ten finned rows (seven with 11%Cr fins, three
with 304SS fins — all 60 fins/ft × 0.05″ thick × ¾″ high, solid, helically wound, continuous
high-frequency weld) and two bare rows. Bank height 9′-2 15/16″ C/C tubes. Tube sheet
61′-7½″ out-to-out; six bays of 10′-3″ between five intermediate tube supports (ASTM A351 HK40
castings, 35 ea, design span 10′-3″, design max temp 1800 °F). Tube lengths: 19 + 9 + 4 = 32 off
62′-2″, plus 65′-3⅛″, 66′-0¾″, 65′-3⅜″ and 66′-0⅝″ at the four connection tubes. Summed
**2,252 ft**. Marks CFT1–CFT4 (finned) and CT1–CT3 (bare) on sheet 03-01.

**Pass count confirmed three ways** — four 4″-600# RTJ flanges (two at inlet, two at outlet);
the Section A end view; and the return arithmetic, 36 tubes − 2 passes = 34 returns against a
Fitting BOM listing exactly 34 (CF2 3 + CF3 1 + CF4 15 + CF5 15).

**Radiant.** Not in this package. The convection outlet ties to the radiant through **re-used
existing crossovers** (noted on sheet 10-10) — the crossovers were not replaced and are not
detailed anywhere in CN-17-605B.

**Separate steam coil — deliberately excluded from the tables above.** Four rows / 12 tubes of
4″ Sch 40 (4.500″ OD × 0.237 wall, ID 4.026″) SA-106 Gr.B with carbon-steel fins at 36 fins/ft,
sitting **above** the process bank (steam inlet EL 36′-4 15/16″, steam outlet EL 38′-1¾″).
Returns are 8 off 4″ Sch 40 S.R. 180° at 8″ C/C (SA-234-WPB); connections are 2 off 4″-300#
RFWN with Sch 40 bore. Surface area 6,371 ft². **Not a process circuit and not pigged** — it is
kept out of Tube Geometry and the Config Rollup so it can never be multiplied into a footage
estimate. Marks CFT5–CFT7 and CT4–CT6.

**Connection notes.** Both sets of flanges sit at the **north / terminal end**, inside the
terminal-end header box, which is bolted and must be opened for access (sheet 10-10:
"REINSTALL EXISTING TERMINAL END HEADER BOX", seal bolting flange with RTV by others). Flow runs
top-down: process enters at the top of the process bank at EL 35′-10″ and leaves at the bottom
at EL 28′-10 13/16″ — counter-current to the rising flue gas. Both elevations are **29–36 ft up**;
whether there is a sound deck with room to change pigs, or scaffold is needed, is unresolved and
is the first access question to ask.

**RTJ, not raised-face.** These are ring-joint grooved flanges. USADebusk launchers are RF —
the customer fabricates the RTJ-to-RF adapters (standard role boundary, confirm in the RFQ).

**Bend limit.** The 30 short-radius cast returns are **8″ centre-to-centre on 4.500″ OD**, a 4″
centreline bend radius — **0.89 × D**, tighter than 1D. The +0.250″ rule sizes pig *diameter*
correctly, but pig *body length* is the binding constraint through these returns. Short-body or
flexible pigs only; validate the geometry against the pig catalogue before committing a load
list.
