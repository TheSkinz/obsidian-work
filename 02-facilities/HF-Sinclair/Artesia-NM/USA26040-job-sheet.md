## H-2421 — Coil Data

| Section | Coils | Pipe OD | Pipe Wall | Pipe ID | Tube Length | Tubes/Coil | Ft/Section |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Convection | 2 | 6.625" | 0.432" | 5.761" | 13.78' avg | 14 | 385.9 cut / ≈427 eff |
| Radiant | 2 | 6.625" | 0.432" | 5.761" | 18.49' avg | 14 | 517.8 cut / ≈559 eff |

Metallurgy: 347 SS (TP347) both sections. Pass config: 2 circuits A→C and B→D, field jumper C↔D loops them into one continuous pig path; heater grand total ≈980–985 ft effective. Connections: nozzles A/B inlets and C/D outlets, all 6"-900# RF, 12× 1-1/8"×7-3/4" bolts, wrench 1-13/16"; launcher piping runs to grade terminating 6"-300#, so **adapters are required** and are HF Sinclair scope. Crossovers are 6" ID constant — no reducer. Effluent: fresh condensate supply from a frac tank; filtration elected (Press 2).

Max pig OD: 6.011" rule cap (5.761 governing ID + 0.250). Final foam 6" / 6.125", the 6.125 as looped-circuit oversized final.
