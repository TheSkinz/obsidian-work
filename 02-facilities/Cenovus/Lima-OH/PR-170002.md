**Tube geometry notes.**

**Convection.** 1142-1-6-3 reads "effective tube length = 68'-8"". P&ID R-14227 reads "CONVECTION: 9 CR + 1/8" CA". Eleven rows are drawn at each end in the section view (2 + 5 + 4), but no tube count is printed. Tube count 22 per pass, 71' average tube and 1,562 ft per pass come from the coworker's structured table (Jesse, 2026-09-19). 71' is overall tube length; the drawing's 68'-8" is effective (fired) length, so the two don't conflict. **OD and wall are now from the drawings** — 1142-1-6-2 labels the convection tubes "4" O.D. × 0.301 M.W." and the finned-tube schedule 1142-4-6-6-A prints the same. That schedule lists 64 finned tubes (FT-1 ×4, FT-2 ×52, FT-3 ×8), all 4" O.D. × 0.301 M.W. A213 Gr. T-9, 11-13%Cr fins 4/in at 0.06" thick, ¾" high except FT-3 at ½"; overall length 70'-3" (FT-1 73'-2⅞") with 69'-7" finned. Against 88 convection tubes that leaves 24 bare, i.e. 16 finned + 6 bare per pass — a division, not a printed figure.

**Radiant segment 1 of 2.** Effective tube length 68'-4" (1142-1-6-3; spot-checked). "RADIANT: 9 CR + 1/8" CA" (R-14227). East and west radiant cells, with wall and roof tubes. 1142-1-6-2 labels the wall tubes "4" O.D. × 0.301 M.W. TUBES, MATERIAL 9Cr-1Mo (A-213 GR. T-9)" at 21 spaces @ 7" = 12'-3", and the roof tubes the same at 8 spaces @ 7" = 4'-8". **Which tubes fall in which segment, and where the size change sits in the flow path, is still on no drawing in hand; the segment order is a placeholder.** The coworker's table gives 31 tubes plus 3 of the larger-bore pipe, 71' each: 34 tubes and 2,414 ft per pass, which matches DSP#25113.1. The table lists the Sch 120 tubes last. That suggests they sit at the outlet, and 1142-1-6-2 draws them at the process-outlet elevation, but no drawing states the pass order.

**Radiant segment 2 of 2.** 1142-1-6-2 labels the bottom radiant tubes "4"-SCH. 120 TUBES, MATERIAL 9Cr-1Mo (A-335-P9)" at 3 spaces @ 12" = 3'-0", drawn at the (4) process outlets, EL 11'-0¾". A335 is a **pipe** spec, so 4" NPS Sch 120 = 4.500" OD, 0.438" wall, 3.624" bore. At 3.624" it is the **larger** bore and carries its own final pig, 3.800", against 3.600" for the 3.398" tube section.

**OD and ID source — corrected 2026-09-28.** The drawings settle what the coworker table got wrong. **1142-1-6-2 Rev H, 1142-4-6-6-A and Waukesha/WF Repco MB-1132-10A-2M all specify the T-9 tubes as 4" O.D. × 0.301" minimum wall.** A213 is a *tube* spec — ordered by actual OD and minimum wall — so OD is **4.000"**, not the 4.500" the coworker table assumed, and the bore is **4.000 − 2 × 0.301 = 3.398"**. **Confirmed by Jesse 2026-09-29: "3.39 is correct."** The drawing gives a minimum wall, which would allow a slightly tighter bore if the mill shipped thicker; that is not how this coil reads in practice, and 3.398" is the working figure. Corroboration: steel drawing 1142-1-6-10 F shows 4½" dia wall-tube holes, and tube-sheet standard 121S-3-137 L states "hole dia in tube sheet and I.D. of sleeve = tube O.D. + ½"" — 4.000 + 0.500 = 4.500, which matches. The return-bend casting is bored and chamfered "for 4-" O.D. × .301" M.W. tubes" and turned to Ø4.000"⁺·⁰⁶²/₋·⁰⁰⁰ at the weld end. **The card previously carried 4.500" OD and a 3.898" ID from the coworker's pipe-data table, which applied the 0.301" tube wall to a pipe OD.** The same table carried the wrong schedules for the Vac's 8", 10" and 12" radiant. The Sch 120 pipe figure is unchanged and agrees with DSP#25113.1's 3.62".

**Return bends — the governing bore.** Waukesha Foundry / WF Repco drawing **MB-1132-10A-2M Rev 1** (pattern 11-3480-X1, Cenovus doc 17-F-22744-01), titled "4" O.D. WELD END U-BEND" and stamped **"PR 170002 COKER FURNACE"**, is a cast ASTM A217 C12 180° return bend on 7.00" ±0.06 C-C, bored and chamfered for 4" O.D. × 0.301" M.W. tubes. It prints **bore Ø3.314" ±0.031"** at the weld end and **Ø3¼" (3.250") through the turn**, R3½. That passage is smaller than the tube bore and smaller than the Sch 120 pipe, so on paper it is the smallest cross-section in the coil. **It is not what sizes the pig** — prior USADebusk campaigns on this heater ran a 3.6" final pig (Jesse, 2026-09-28), and that field result governs over the drawing figure. The sheet carries a red annotation, **"APPLIES TO RETURN BENDS INSTALLED PRIOR TO 2019 ONLY"** — some bends were replaced in or after 2019 and **their bore is unknown**; nothing in hand says which tubes or what size. Customer order 8400652914, customer named as "Huskey Lima Refining", drawn 10/7/2011, checked 5/20/2013, marked PRELIMINARY with a 5/24/13 approval signature; qty 6 against order item 1.

**Return bends.** Drawn as 180° welded U-bends inside 3/16"-plate header boxes, BOM items on 1142-1-6-4, which we do not have. No plug headers are drawn. Cast or wrought is not stated.

**Final pig sizes — settled 2026-09-28 (Jesse). Each tube size carries its own max pig size; this heater has two.**

| Section | ID | Final pig |
|---|---|---|
| Convection + radiant wall/roof, 4" OD × 0.301 MW tube | 3.398" | **3.600"** |
| Radiant bottom, 4" Sch 120 pipe | 3.624" | **3.800"** |

**How the two sizes are run** (Jesse, 2026-09-28): the 3.800" is launched from the **radiant outlet** and run up the 3.624" Sch 120 section only, **until it reaches the reducer** at the step down to the 3.398" tube; it then **reverses and runs back to the launcher**. It never enters the smaller section. The 3.600" covers the tube section. Bi-directional within one size segment, so no pig has to pass a size step.

USADebusk has pigged this Coker before and both sizes come from those campaigns. The 3.800" recorded on 2026-09-19 was **not** superseded by the OD correction — it belongs to the Sch 120 section and stands; the correction added the 3.600" for the tube section, which the card had been missing because it carried the wrong tube bore. The cast return bend's printed 3.250" turn passage (MB-1132-10A-2M) is a drawing figure for the pre-2019 bends; prior campaigns have run these pigs through this coil, and that field result governs.

Steam-injection inlets are 3"-600# RFWN, A182 F9. Inlets and outlets are both on the T end at platforms A and C. Tube removal is at the Z end. "FIELD WELD (4) PLACES" appears at the splice line, EL 18'-2", Z end. Decoking spools (4) are shown on the charge lines.
