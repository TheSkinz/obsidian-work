**Tube geometry notes.**

**Convection.** 1142-1-6-3 reads "effective tube length = 68'-8"". P&ID R-14227 reads "CONVECTION: 9 CR + 1/8" CA". Eleven rows are drawn at each end in the section view (2 + 5 + 4), but no tube count is printed. **Neither drawing states tube OD, schedule or ID**; see the source paragraph below. Tube count 22 per pass, 71' average tube and 1,562 ft per pass come from the coworker's structured table (Jesse, 2026-09-19). 71' is overall tube length; the drawing's 68'-8" is effective (fired) length, so the two don't conflict.

**Radiant segment 1 of 2.** Effective tube length 68'-4" (1142-1-6-3; spot-checked). "RADIANT: 9 CR + 1/8" CA" (R-14227). East and west radiant cells, with wall and roof tubes. The 3.90" / 3.62" split is DSP#25113.1's and is unconfirmed. **Which tubes are 3.90" and which 3.62", and where the change falls in the flow path, is on no drawing in hand; the segment order is a placeholder.** The coworker's table gives 31 tubes at 3.898" (2,201 ft) plus 3 at 3.624" (213 ft), 71' each: 34 tubes and 2,414 ft per pass, which matches DSP#25113.1. The table lists the 3.624" tubes last. That suggests they sit at the outlet, but nothing states it.

**Radiant segment 2 of 2.** The 3.624" bore (Sch 120). It governs pig sizing; the final pig is 3.8".

**OD, wall and ID source.** 4.500" OD, 0.301" wall → 3.898", and 4.500" Sch 120, 0.438" wall → 3.624", come from a coworker's pipe-data table supplied by Jesse on 2026-09-19. The table doesn't say where they came from, and no Cenovus drawing in hand states them. They agree with DSP#25113.1's 3.90" / 3.62" and are arithmetically consistent. The same table carried the wrong schedules for the Vac's 8", 10" and 12" radiant, so **these stay unconfirmed** until 1142-2-6-5 / 1142-1-6-4 or a field-measured clean ID settles them.

**Return bends.** Drawn as 180° welded U-bends inside 3/16"-plate header boxes, BOM items on 1142-1-6-4, which we do not have. No plug headers are drawn. Cast or wrought is not stated.
