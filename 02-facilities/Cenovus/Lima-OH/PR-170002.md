**Tube geometry notes.**

**Convection.** 1142-1-6-3 reads "effective tube length = 68'-8"". P&ID R-14227 reads "CONVECTION: 9 CR + 1/8" CA". Eleven rows are drawn at each end in the section view (2 + 5 + 4), but no tube count is printed. Tube count 22 per pass, 71' average tube and 1,562 ft per pass come from the coworker's structured table (Jesse, 2026-09-19). 71' is overall tube length; the drawing's 68'-8" is effective (fired) length, so the two don't conflict. **OD and wall are now from the drawings** — 1142-1-6-2 labels the convection tubes "4" O.D. × 0.301 M.W." and the finned-tube schedule 1142-4-6-6-A prints the same. That schedule lists 64 finned tubes (FT-1 ×4, FT-2 ×52, FT-3 ×8), all 4" O.D. × 0.301 M.W. A213 Gr. T-9, 11-13%Cr fins 4/in at 0.06" thick, ¾" high except FT-3 at ½"; overall length 70'-3" (FT-1 73'-2⅞") with 69'-7" finned. Against 88 convection tubes that leaves 24 bare, i.e. 16 finned + 6 bare per pass — a division, not a printed figure.

**Radiant segment 1 of 2.** Effective tube length 68'-4" (1142-1-6-3; spot-checked). "RADIANT: 9 CR + 1/8" CA" (R-14227). East and west radiant cells, with wall and roof tubes. 1142-1-6-2 labels the wall tubes "4" O.D. × 0.301 M.W. TUBES, MATERIAL 9Cr-1Mo (A-213 GR. T-9)" at 21 spaces @ 7" = 12'-3", and the roof tubes the same at 8 spaces @ 7" = 4'-8". **Which tubes fall in which segment, and where the size change sits in the flow path, is still on no drawing in hand; the segment order is a placeholder.** The coworker's table gives 31 tubes plus 3 of the larger-bore pipe, 71' each: 34 tubes and 2,414 ft per pass, which matches DSP#25113.1. The table lists the Sch 120 tubes last. That suggests they sit at the outlet, and 1142-1-6-2 draws them at the process-outlet elevation, but no drawing states the pass order.

**Radiant segment 2 of 2.** 1142-1-6-2 labels the bottom radiant tubes "4"-SCH. 120 TUBES, MATERIAL 9Cr-1Mo (A-335-P9)" at 3 spaces @ 12" = 3'-0", drawn at the (4) process outlets, EL 11'-0¾". A335 is a **pipe** spec, so 4" NPS Sch 120 = 4.500" OD, 0.438" wall, 3.624" bore. At 3.624" it is the **larger** bore; the 3.398" tube section governs the final pig.

**OD and ID source — corrected 2026-09-28.** The drawings settle what the coworker table got wrong. **1142-1-6-2 Rev H, 1142-4-6-6-A and Waukesha/WF Repco MB-1132-10A-2M all specify the T-9 tubes as 4" O.D. × 0.301" minimum wall.** A213 is a *tube* spec — ordered by actual OD and minimum wall — so OD is **4.000"**, not the 4.500" the coworker table assumed, and the bore is **4.000 − 2 × 0.301 = 3.398" or less**; minimum wall means the mill may ship thicker, so the as-built ID is at most 3.398". Corroboration: steel drawing 1142-1-6-10 F shows 4½" dia wall-tube holes, and tube-sheet standard 121S-3-137 L states "hole dia in tube sheet and I.D. of sleeve = tube O.D. + ½"" — 4.000 + 0.500 = 4.500, which matches. The return-bend casting is bored and chamfered "for 4-" O.D. × .301" M.W. tubes" and turned to Ø4.000"⁺·⁰⁶²/₋·⁰⁰⁰ at the weld end. **The card previously carried 4.500" OD and a 3.898" ID from the coworker's pipe-data table, which applied the 0.301" tube wall to a pipe OD.** The same table carried the wrong schedules for the Vac's 8", 10" and 12" radiant. The Sch 120 pipe figure is unchanged and agrees with DSP#25113.1's 3.62". A field-measured clean ID is still the only number that reflects 30+ years of service.

**Return bends — the governing bore.** Waukesha Foundry / WF Repco drawing **MB-1132-10A-2M Rev 1** (pattern 11-3480-X1, Cenovus doc 17-F-22744-01), titled "4" O.D. WELD END U-BEND" and stamped **"PR 170002 COKER FURNACE"**, is a cast ASTM A217 C12 180° return bend on 7.00" ±0.06 C-C, bored and chamfered for 4" O.D. × 0.301" M.W. tubes. It prints **bore Ø3.314" ±0.031"** at the weld end and **Ø3¼" (3.250") through the turn**, R3½. That passage is smaller than the tube bore and smaller than the Sch 120 pipe, so on paper it is the smallest cross-section in the coil. **It is not what sizes the pig** — prior USADebusk campaigns on this heater ran a 3.6" final pig (Jesse, 2026-09-28), and that field result governs over the drawing figure. The sheet carries a red annotation, **"APPLIES TO RETURN BENDS INSTALLED PRIOR TO 2019 ONLY"** — some bends were replaced in or after 2019 and **their bore is unknown**; nothing in hand says which tubes or what size. Customer order 8400652914, customer named as "Huskey Lima Refining", drawn 10/7/2011, checked 5/20/2013, marked PRELIMINARY with a 5/24/13 approval signature; qty 6 against order item 1.

**Return bends.** Drawn as 180° welded U-bends inside 3/16"-plate header boxes, BOM items on 1142-1-6-4, which we do not have. No plug headers are drawn. Cast or wrought is not stated.
