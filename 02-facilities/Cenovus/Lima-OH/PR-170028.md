**Tube geometry notes.**

**Convection.** 13-F-07937: 144 process tubes (8 passes × 18), all 6⅝" OD × Sch 80S SMLS, 76'-9" overall tube length typical (76'-1" finned, 75'-9" O/O tubesheet), with longer terminal tubes at 81'-0" to 82'-0⅝". From the top: 8 finned rows (solid fins, 11-13Cr, 1" high × 0.0625", 4/in), 7 finned rows (11-13Cr, ¾" high × 0.0625", 3/in), 3 bare shock rows. One bare row of 4½" steam-superheater tube sits between the finned blocks and is a utility coil, not process. The row-to-BOM-item mapping in the middle rows is uncertain. Return bends CF2, 16 per pass; one jumper per pass (Mk 03A, two 90° elbows) ships loose for field install at the 0° return end. Length/Circuit 1,386 ft is DSP#25113.1's figure and equals 18 × 77 ft; the straight-tube BOM sum is ≈1,392 ft, within the row-mapping uncertainty.

**Radiant.** 13-F-07935 tube BOM: RT1 8 × 78'-1½" (inlet), RT2 160 × 72'-0", RT3 8 × 77'-7⅝" (outlet): 176 tubes = 8 passes × 22, all 8⅝" OD × Sch 80S. Length/Circuit is the BOM sum for one pass, 78.125 + 20 × 72 + 77.635 = 1,596 ft straight tube (Jesse, 2026-09-19). It excludes the 21 return bends and RT4, the 2'-3¼" outlet spool piece. **DSP#25113.1 carried 1,738 ft, which no length on the drawing reproduces; 1,596 supersedes it.** Return bends RF2, 168 total, 21 per pass. The middle bend of each pass ships loose for field weld. The coil is built as upper and lower halves (Mk 01A/01B), horizontal tubes stacked vertically, 1'-4" spacing.

**IDs.** Neither drawing prints an ID. 5.761" and 7.625" are the standard pipe-table bores for 6" and 8" Sch 80S against the stated OD and schedule (Jesse, 2026-09-19). DSP#25113.1 carried the same values.

**Max pig OD caveat.** Governing ID is the convection's 5.761". Adding 0.250" gives 6.011", rounded down to **6.000"**, the nearest 1/8" size under the ceiling. The radiant is 7.625", so a pig sized to the convection runs undersized through the radiant. The crossover's 6"-to-8" transition is where pig sizes change.

Pass inlets on 4305-R-00100 are 6"-13-P-0125 through -0132 with 8×6 reducers and 6" CSO valves. Radiant inlets sit at the top of the radiant, EL 42'-1".
