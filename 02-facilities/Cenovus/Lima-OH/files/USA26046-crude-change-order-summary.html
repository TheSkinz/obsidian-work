How smart pigging hours are recorded

The service receipt has no separate box for smart pigging. Pump time is entered under Pigging Hours, and the shift summary records each task with its clock times. Smart pigging hours are taken from those clock times.

The Trimax runs three passes at once, one per pump, so one pass can be on the inspection tool while the other two are still being cleaned.
