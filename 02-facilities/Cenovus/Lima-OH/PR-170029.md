**Tube geometry notes.**

**Convection.** 13-F-08225 process tube BOM: 88 tubes (8 passes × 11), 4½" OD × 0.25" **minimum** wall SMLS: 8 finned rows (fins 11-13Cr, 1" high × 0.0625", 4/in) and 3 bare rows, 8 tubes per row. 40'-3" overall typical, terminal tubes 43'-3½" to 44'-4⅛". Length/Circuit is the BOM sum per pass (VCT1, VCT2, VCFT3–5). It excludes VCT6, one 3'-0" piece per pass. The wall column is the printed minimum wall, not a schedule. ID 4.000" is OD − 2 × minimum wall, so the true bore is **no larger** than 4.000". DSP#25113.1 carried 462 ft, per pass, with bends. The steam-generation and BFW rows are SA106 Gr. B utility coils, not process.

**Radiant segment 1 of 5.** Inlet end. Tube BOM VRT16–VRT20: 32 tubes = 4 per pass, 4" NPS Sch 80S, 46'-11⅝" to 53'-10 5/16" total length (each tube is two pieces plus a center weld). Radiant inlet is field-welded 4", at EL 14'-6" for passes 1, 4, 5, 8 and EL 13'-6" for passes 2, 3, 6, 7.

**Radiant segment 2 of 5.** VRT10, 12, 13, 15: 16 tubes = 2 per pass, 6" NPS Sch 80S.

**Radiant segment 3 of 5.** VRT8: 8 tubes = 1 per pass, **8" NPS Sch 60**, 45'-4⅛". The field jumpers (01E/01F) carry an 8"×6" reducer. DSP#25113.1 gave 7.625" here, the Sch 80 bore; the drawing says Sch 60.

**Radiant segment 4 of 5.** VRT5, VRT7: 8 tubes = 1 per pass, 10" NPS Sch 80S. DSP#25113.1's 9.562" is the Sch 80 bore.

**Radiant segment 5 of 5.** Outlet end. VRT1, VRT2: 8 tubes = 1 per pass, 12" NPS Sch 80S. DSP#25113.1's 11.374" is the Sch 80 bore.

**Radiant, whole section.** Note 8 says "coil shown equals (2) passes" and Note 9 that BOM quantities are for all 8 passes. Tubes numbered #1–#18 on the elevation are two passes of 9. Concentric reducers sit at every size step. Length/Circuit per segment is the BOM sum per pass, 432.4 ft over 9 tubes. The short connector pieces are excluded: VRT3, 4, 6, 9, 11, 14, 21 and 22, about 14.5 ft per pass. DSP#25113.1 carried 446 ft, which matches the sum with those pieces included. The BOM and the rev-8 clouded elevation disagree on several B-piece lengths (VRT1B 28'-7 5/16" vs 29'-5 5/16"; VRT5B 19'-1⅝" vs 19'-3⅛"), under 2 ft per pass. The BOM was used. Terminal tubes carry an extra 12" that isn't in the BOM (Note 10).

**IDs.** No drawing prints an ID. The radiant bores are standard pipe-table values for the printed NPS and schedule (Jesse, 2026-09-19).
