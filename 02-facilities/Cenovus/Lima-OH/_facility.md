## Site Equipment and Constraints

Per the quotes, Cenovus provides ULSD for all equipment, clean water with a 1½" fire hose and Y-connection, radios, 55-gal waste drums, a 750 CFM compressor with 2" bull hose, launcher and receiver spools, 110V/20A power, pipefitting and scaffolding, gaskets and bolt-ups, vac-truck decon, hose crossings, light towers, containment berms and vehicle passes.
