## Field Notes

**Pigging path, from the Job Note's own tie-point list.** Four coils in series per section, both spools at the same end, the 180s taking the other.

| Section | Jumper spools (BP installs) | Blinded | Launcher / Receiver |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-1A | TP-08↔09, TP-12↔13, TP-14↔15 | TP-11 | TP-05, TP-06 |
| F-3A | TP-17↔18, TP-21↔22, TP-23↔24 | TP-20, TP-25 | TP-26, TP-27 |

**Execution sequence per BP's procedure.** Fill and flush → high-speed flush and initial flow test → sizing foam to determine starting pig size → increasing pig sizes until the operators determine the tubes are clean → oversized polishing pig → smart pig by Steady Flux → possible swab to push air out of the line → demobilize → BP removes temporary spools and 4" blinds and reinstalls the F-1A/F-3A headers at TP-11 through TP-25.

**⚠ Blind size conflict, unresolved.** Job Note p.2 instructs *"Install 10" Blind"* at TP-11, TP-20 and TP-25. Drawing D-1037-K-3126 notes *"TP-11, TP-20, & TP-25 shall be blinded with a 4" - Cl300 Blind Flange per 03CS-2PW."* The headers are 10" with 10"×4" weld-o-lets, so 4" at the branch is the reading that reconciles — **but this is BP's scope and the contradiction is theirs to settle.** Confirm before rig-in rather than finding the wrong blind fitted.

**Spool bore.** Temporary pigging spools are 4" Sch STD (ID 4.026") into 4" Sch 80 tube (ID 3.826"). Larger into smaller is the safe direction; the tube governs pig sizing, not the spool.

**Access.** Grade EL 18'-0". Absorber outlets EL 37'-4¹¹⁄₁₆", desulfurizer inlets EL 36'-9¾", desulfurizer outlets EL 35'-1" — connections roughly 17–19 ft above grade. Launcher barrel must land within 48" of grade or deck with >36" clearance for the lid swing. Confirm a working deck at all four launcher positions on the walk.

**BP prerequisite work.** Header removal at TP-11 through TP-25 and temporary spool bolt-up are BP scope and must be complete before USADebusk can rig in. This is the single item most likely to convert rig-in into stand-by.

## Notes

**Verification.** Nothing on this card is field-verified. Tube counts, lengths, schedule and metallurgy are read from the 1986–1989 Radco/Pritchard as-built drawings attached to Job Note 40379977; the coil was modified under that project (D-1037-K-3126 shows "new coil layout" with two new rows of tubes), so the tube bills read here are the **as-modified** configuration. **Tube IDs are computed from stated OD and schedule, not stated on the drawings.** Flange sizes and ratings are from the Job Note and the inspection isometric. Launcher elevation is inferred from plant elevations on D-1037-K-0970 against grade EL 18'-0" and has not been measured.

**Duty.** F-1A 64.8 MM BTU/HR, F-3A 20.8 MM BTU/HR (P&ID D-1037-G-0044). Operating conditions from the Job Note: F-1A 320 PSIG at 400°F, F-3A 330 PSIG at 490°F. Design: F-1A 375 PSIG at 650°F, F-3A 300 PSIG at 522°F. Design code ASME B31.3.

**Metallurgy.** A335-P9 is 9Cr-1Mo, an alloy rather than carbon steel, and the fins are 11% Cr. Nothing in the BP package calls for passivation and none is in scope.
