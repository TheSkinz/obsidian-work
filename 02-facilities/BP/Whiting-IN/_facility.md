## Site Safety and Procedures

- **A BP representative must be on site during any pigging.** Stated in the Job Note's general notes, not a courtesy.
- Temporary pigging spool material spec is **03CS-2PW**: 4" pipe, seamless, Sch STD, ASME B36.10M, ASTM A106-B. Shop welds per WBU 01-01-SM-SM-AE-01, in accordance with WBU-GIS 18-1010 and 42-1011, preheat 50°F minimum, max interpass 600°F, 100% VT and 5% RT, hydrotest 1,125 PSIG for 15 minutes minimum.
- Flange torque categories: **10" pipe Category II, 4" pipe Category III.**
- Field materials per 03CS-2PW: ASTM A193 Gr. B7 studs, ASTM A194 Gr. 2H nuts, GK-25 Lamons Style WRI gaskets or BP-approved equal per WBU-GIS 42-0010.
- Spool fabrication, header removal and reinstallation, and blind removal/reinstallation are **BP scope** — USADebusk supplies pressure hoses, pig launchers/receivers and the pigging equipment.
